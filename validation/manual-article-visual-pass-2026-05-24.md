# Manual Article Visual Pass - 2026-05-24

## Scope

- Comparison: local Hugo preview at `http://localhost:1313` versus live site at `https://www.rhino-inquisitor.com`.
- Method: manual browser review, one article at a time. No automated visual diff or comparison script determines the verdict.
- Target set: 150 article routes under `src/content/posts`.
- Primary checks: visual aberrations, content mismatches, lost content, media/caption parity, code/table/embed readability.
- Intentional redesign differences in the global shell are noted only when they obscure, remove, or materially alter article content.
- Owner decision: improved article titles and improved link labels are accepted changes and count as Pass unless they change meaning, point to the wrong target, or hide lost content.
- Owner decision: additive content improvements, added captions, expanded labels, and expanded local presentation count as Pass when the original content is still present and readable. The audit's main goal is loss detection.

## Verdict Legend

- Pass: no article-content issue observed.
- Hold: article is readable, but one or more visual/content differences need owner review or correction.
- Fail: missing, wrong, or unusable article content observed.
