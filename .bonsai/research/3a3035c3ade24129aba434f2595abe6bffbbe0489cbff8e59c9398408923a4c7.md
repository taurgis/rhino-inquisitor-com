## ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini Have Different Tells

Model families have different tells. Across all nine models, 65% of tells are unique to one model family.

The em dash is the best-known tell, but AI models no longer overuse it consistently. GPT-6 Astra uses em dashes about one-eighth as often as human writers, Claude Opus 5 uses them at about the human rate, and Gemini 3.1 Pro has nearly stopped using them.

Source: Graphite research, AI Tells (2026). The analysis uses 10,000 human articles and 90,000 AI-generated articles from nine models on the same topics. Mean em dashes per 1,000 words across the 9,984 aligned topics.
