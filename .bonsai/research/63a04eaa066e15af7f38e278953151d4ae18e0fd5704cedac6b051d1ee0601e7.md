## Compressed

### Test and go live with Giving

Test your integration by doing the following:

1. [Create and activate a test donation campaign].
2. Use our [test cards] to make a donation.
3. Get the [**DONATION** webhook] for the donation.

When you are ready to go live with Giving, enable the [Adyen Giving merchant webhook], and [create and activate a live donation campaign] in your [live Customer Area].

When you make live donations, you can verify the outcome by either:

* Finding the donation in a [Giving report or dashboard].
* Getting the [**DONATION** webhook] for the donation.

### Test and go live with Giving

Test your integration by doing the following:

1. [Create and activate a test donation campaign](/online-payments/donations/testing/#create-and-activate-a-test-donation-campaign).
2. Use our [test cards](/online-payments/donations/testing/#test-cards) to make a donation.
3. Get the [**DONATION** webhook](/development-resources/webhooks/webhook-types#other-webhooks) for the donation.

When you are ready to go live with Giving, enable the [Adyen Giving merchant webhook](/development-resources/webhooks/webhook-types#other-webhooks), and [create and activate a live donation campaign](/online-payments/donations/testing/#create-and-activate-a-live-donation-campaign) in your [live Customer Area](https://ca-live.adyen.com/).

When you make live donations, you can verify the outcome by either:

* Finding the donation in a [Giving report or dashboard](/reporting/monitor-donations).
* Getting the [**DONATION** webhook](/development-resources/webhooks/webhook-types#other-webhooks) for the donation.
