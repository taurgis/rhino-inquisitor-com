### C.5 Estimating TV Distance

In §[4](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.11156v4#S4 "4 Hardness of Reliable AI Text Detection ‣ Can AI-Generated Text be Reliably Detected?"), we show experiments supporting the assumption that more advanced LLMs lead to smaller TV distance between human and machine text distributions. We present two experimental settings — (i) Using synthetic text data and (ii) Using projection. In Figure [15](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.11156v4#A3.F15 "Figure 15 ‣ C.5 Estimating TV Distance ‣ Appendix C Proofs and Corollaries ‣ Can AI-Generated Text be Reliably Detected?"), we show the TV distances computed with varying vocabulary sizes and sequence lengths. In all the experiments, we consistently find that the TV distances reduce as the network size increases.
