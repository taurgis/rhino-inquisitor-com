# Share B2C Commerce Einstein Data

B2C Commerce Einstein Search Dictionaries is the only Einstein feature that enables you to share feature-specific data across the B2C Commerce merchant community.

### Required Editions

| Available in: B2C Commerce |
| --- |

Note To accept this agreement, you consent to contributing your search dictionary data to a shared database for the purposes of providing recommendations to other merchants who have also consented to contributing to the shared database. You further acknowledge that Salesforce can access your search dictionary data submitted to B2C Commerce for the purpose of training and improving the Einstein Search Dictionary and any other of our current and future similar or related features, products or services.

To contribute your search dictionary data to the shared database:

1.  In Business Manager, click App Launcher , and then select **Administration** | **Global Preferences** | **Einstein Search Dictionaries Opt-In**.
2.  Select **I accept these terms and conditions**, and click **Save**.
