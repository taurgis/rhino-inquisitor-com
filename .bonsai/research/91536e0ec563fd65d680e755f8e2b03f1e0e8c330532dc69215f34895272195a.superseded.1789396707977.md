## Summary

Third-Party Applications > Facebook and Instagram Shop Ads Integration > Prerequisites

## Compressed

### Prerequisites

Before you integrate with Facebook and Instagram:

1. Contact B2C Commerce Support to enable the Facebook feature toggle.
2. Make sure you have met the [SFCC Prerequisites].
3. Create a [Meta Developer Account].
4. Optional: Create a [Meta Business Account].
   - This step is optional because you can add your developer account to a pre-existing business account before starting the onboarding process.
5. Make sure you have:
   - A Salesforce B2C Commerce site catalog
   - A Salesforce B2C Commerce business user account

## Detailed

### Prerequisites

Before you integrate with Facebook and Instagram:

1. Contact B2C Commerce Support to enable the Facebook feature toggle.
2. Make sure you have met the [SFCC Prerequisites](https://github.com/SalesforceCommerceCloud/social_channel_integrations?tab=readme-ov-file#prerequisites).
3. Create a [Meta Developer Account](https://developers.facebook.com/).
4. Optional: Create a [Meta Business Account](https://business.facebook.com/).
   - This step is optional because you can add your developer account to a pre-existing business account before starting the onboarding process.
5. Make sure you have:
   - A Salesforce B2C Commerce site catalog
   - A Salesforce B2C Commerce business user account
