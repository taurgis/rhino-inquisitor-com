## Compressed

### Configure Permalink Checkout URLs for Social Sites

Send shoppers from Facebook and Instagram to your B2C Commerce storefront by adding checkout URLs.

:::important Create permalinks only for SFRA and hybrid storefronts. :::

1. In your Meta Commerce Business product catalog, make sure that your product IDs (called content IDs in Meta) match your SKU IDs.

2. Enable permalinks.

   1. In Business Manager, from App Launcher , go to **Administration** > **Global Preferences** > **Feature Switches**.
   2. Select **Enable Permalinks Integration**.

3. Add a URL pipeline to route shoppers to checkout.
   :::note
   Add a storefront link for simple and variant products, and each cart can include one coupon. Links can’t be created for a bundle, option, bonus, subscriptions, or gift card product. All transactions must be in US currency.
   :::
   1. Select **Merchant Tools** > **Site** > **SEO** > **URL Rules**.
   2. Go to the **Pipeline URLs** tab.
   3. For New Alias, enter a term to represent the pipeline in the URL. For example, to show the URL as hostnamealias/s/sitename/permalink-checkout instead of hostnamealias/s/sitename/Checkout-Start, enter permalink-checkout.
   4. For Resolves To, enter **PermalinkCheckout-Begin**.
      
   5. Click **Apply**.
