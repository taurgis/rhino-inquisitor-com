## What Are B2C Commerce Solutions on AgentExchange?

You’re a Salesforce customer with an implementation that includes Salesforce B2C Commerce.

Enter B2C Commerce solutions on AgentExchange.

[AgentExchange] is Salesforce’s enterprise cloud marketplace with solutions and consultants to maximize and extend your Salesforce environment. And many trusted partners have created great B2C Commerce solutions on AgentExchange.

With Salesforce B2C Commerce solutions on AgentExchange, you get:

*   An accelerated time to market  
    
*   Low-risk ecommerce implementations  
    
*   Lower total cost of ownership  
    
*   Innovation with speed  
    
*   Trust at scale  
    

On AgentExchange, there are two types of B2C Commerce solutions for you to consider: B2C Commerce cartridges and headless integrations.

## B2C Commerce Cartridges

A cartridge is a container for packaging and deploying program code and data.

*   Generic cartridges contain reusable business functionality that you can deploy to many sites.  
    
*   Application-specific cartridges contain merchant-specific and site-specific functionality.  
    

Cartridges are pretty cool: They can provide third-party functionality such as tax calculation or credit card processing.

## Headless Integrations

A headless integration is an API-first implementation pattern that decouples your ecommerce frontend and backend.

There are two types of headless integrations: headless integration (API-only) and composable storefront.

| Headless Integration | Application | How It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headless Integration (API-only) | Backend | Integrates with and uses other Commerce APIs on functions like data synchronization. |
| Composable Storefrontor Progressive Web Application (PWA) Kit | Frontend | Runs on Salesforce Managed Runtime and adds functionality by combining Commerce and third-party APIs. Composable storefront components are available for customers who use PWA Kit. |

## B2C Commerce Solution Use Cases

Now that you know a bit more about B2C Commerce solutions, let’s look at some specific ways they can help your storefront.

*   **Retain customers and secure recurring revenue.** Implement loyalty, gift card, and subscription programs.  
    
*   **Create profitable, long-lasting customer relationships**. Use AI to better target shoppers with personalization, and to retarget abandoned cart scenarios.  
    
*   **Exceed shipping and fulfillment targets**. Accurately provide shipping, fulfillment, and pricing options.  
    
*   **Prevent fraud**. Secure your marketplace and ward against nefarious bot attacks.  
    
*   **Secure payments and accurately pay tax**. Calculate and pay sales tax and other required taxes in any region in the world.  
    
*   **Move faster with flexible storefronts**. Provide a fast, app-like experience on the web with headless architecture.  
    

## Find B2C Commerce Solutions on AgentExchange

So you’re ready to get some great, prebuilt, plug-and-play B2C Commerce solutions for your marketplace. It’s easy to find B2C Commerce solutions for your business on AgentExchange.

1.  From the AgentExchange home page, click **Collections**.  
    
2.  Click **Salesforce Foundations**.  
    

It’s worthwhile to explore all Agentforce Commerce collections on the Salesforce Foundations page, but what you’re focusing on today are B2C Commerce and headless integrations.

Scan the listing tiles for B2C cartridges or headless integrations that meet your business needs.

1.  To read more about the solution’s offering and pricing structure, or to read customer reviews, click a listing tile.  
    
2.  When you click **See more**, you’re redirected to an AgentExchange search page that displays all B2C Commerce cartridges or headless integrations.  
    
3.  On the search page, you can further refine your search using the filters, such as Business Need or Solution Type.
