## Compressed

### Biases in content

#### Pro-authoritarian bias

Even large American frontier AI models have for a number of prompts (as of 2026) a pro-authoritarian bias, due in part to how it is trained on the entirety of the internet (and does not exclude unreliable sources on political topics such as propaganda from state media). Model makers also cite safety for their users in authoritarian countries as another reason.[\[33\]][\[34\]] Chinese language responses are also more authoritarian than English-language ones, for example.[\[33\]] Models were also more likely to criticize governments in freer countries than more repressive regimes, sometimes citing safety concerns.[\[33\]]
