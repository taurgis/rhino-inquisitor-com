## Summary

Set up the cartridge > Step 5: Set up webhooks > 2. Add webhook credentials to the Salesforce Business Manager

## Compressed

### 2. Add webhook credentials to the Salesforce Business Manager

1. In the Salesforce Business Manager, go to **Merchant tools** > **Site Preferences** > **Adyen Settings** > **Webhook settings**.
2. Enter the **Webhook username** and **Webhook password** from your [Customer Area].
3. Optionally, enter your **HMAC Key**.
4. Select **Save changes**.

## Detailed

### 2. Add webhook credentials to the Salesforce Business Manager

1. In the Salesforce Business Manager, go to **Merchant tools** > **Site Preferences** > **Adyen Settings** > **Webhook settings**.
2. Enter the **Webhook username** and **Webhook password** from your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/).
3. Optionally, enter your **HMAC Key**.
4. Select **Save changes**.
