As with the semantic operation analysis (Appendix [H](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15597#A8 "Appendix H Semantic Operation Analysis ‣ LLMs Corrupt Your Documents When You Delegate")), we use single-round-trip results from the edit testing phase (Appendix [K.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15597#A11.SS6 "K.6 Stage 6: Edit Task Quality Assurance ‣ Appendix K Dataset Creation Process ‣ LLMs Corrupt Your Documents When You Delegate")) to isolate document-level effects from multi-round error accumulation.
