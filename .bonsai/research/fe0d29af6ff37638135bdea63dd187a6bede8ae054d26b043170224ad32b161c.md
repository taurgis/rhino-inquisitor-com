## Detailed

### 3.3 Performance by Domain

We compare the performance of Pangram against GPTZero and Originality on 10 domains of text: blog posts, books, creative writing, email, news, Q&A, reviews, scientific writing, student writing, and Wikipedia. Creative writing is taken mostly from the Reddit WritingPrompts subreddit ([Fan et al., 2018](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib17)) as well as the Ghostbusters subset ([Verma et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib18)). Books are taken from Project Gutenberg. Email is taken from the Enron email dataset ([Enron Corp and Cohen, 2015](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib19)). QA is taken from a mix of sources, including Reddit ELI5, AskHistorians, AskScience ([Fan et al., 2019](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib20)), as well as the open source MedQUAD ([Asma and Demner-Fushman, 2019](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib21)) and FIQA datasets ([fiq, 2018](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib22)). Student writing is taken from a mix of sources, including the PERSUADE corpus ([University et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib23)), the Liang benchmark ([Liang et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib8)), and college admissions essays collected from various sources around the web. Reviews are taken from Amazon, Google Maps, and Yelp directly. In all cases, human data is verified to have been written prior to 2021 to avoid contamination from AI writing.
