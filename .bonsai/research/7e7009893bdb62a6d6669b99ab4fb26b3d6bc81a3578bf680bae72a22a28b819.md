## Summary

Required conditions for origin trials > What about the workers?

## Compressed

### What about the workers?

Origin trials features can be made available to service workers, shared workers, and dedicated workers. To enable access for service workers and shared workers, you must provide a token in an `Origin-Trial` header.

Dedicated workers inherit access to features enabled by their parent document.
