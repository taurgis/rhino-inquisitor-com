## Step 5: Set up webhooks

To inform your Commerce Cloud store of payment status changes, Adyen uses [webhooks], sent as HTTP callbacks to endpoints on your server. To receive webhooks, you need to enter your server's details in the Customer Area, and add the webhooks credentials to the Salesforce Business Manager.

## Detailed

## Step 5: Set up webhooks

To inform your Commerce Cloud store of payment status changes, Adyen uses [webhooks](/development-resources/webhooks), sent as HTTP callbacks to endpoints on your server. To receive webhooks, you need to enter your server's details in the Customer Area, and add the webhooks credentials to the Salesforce Business Manager.
