## 4\. Findings

Overall, 118118 participants completed four tasks each, resulting in a dataset of 472472 essays. Among these participants, 6565 received AI-generated suggestions to aid their writing—3636 in India and 2929 in the US. These participants saw a total of 12,015 suggestions, accepting 1,476 of them (12.3%12.3\\%). Of the rejected suggestions, only 0.6%0.6\\% were explicitly rejected by pressing the escape key; the rest were dismissed by continuing to type on. Further, a majority of the rejected suggestions (68.8%68.8\\%) were dismissed within 500 ms of appearing on the screen, indicating that they were dismissed in the flow of writing, perhaps without being fully considered by the user. These phases represent bursts of focused manual typing where AI suggestions may not be desired ([Buschek et al., 2021](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.11360#bib.bib14)). The most common suggestions provided by the model are shown in Table [4](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.11360#S4.T4 "Table 4 ‣ 4.
