## Google Channels Integration

Use the Google Channels integration to list your local store product availability on Google Surfaces. If you use Salesforce Omnichannel Inventory(OCI), list inventory directly from your OCI service. Inventory exposure on Google Surfaces improves product discoverability, and drives foot traffic to your local stores.
