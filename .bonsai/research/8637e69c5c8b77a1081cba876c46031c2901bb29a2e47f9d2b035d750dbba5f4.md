## Compressed

### Outdated _access-date_ parameters

In some AI-assisted text, citations may include an _access-date_ by default, but the date can look unexpectedly old relative to when the edit was made (for example, an article created in December 2025 containing multiple citations with `|access-date=12 December 2024`). However, newer chatbots seldom produce this error, and older _access-date_ values can occur legitimately (copied citations, offline work, batch moves/merges).
