## Compressed

…erplexity, and more ChatGPT markers

Near-zero-false-positive markup that LLM answer UIs leak into pasted text, per Wikipedia's Signs of AI writing (Markup section):

- openai: attributableIndex, the invisible U+E200-U+E203 private-use
  citation delimiters (as chars), the stripped-delimiter citeturn0search0
  form, and utm\_source=chatgpt.com reference URLs
- gemini: \[cite: 1\], \[span\_1\] / (end\_span)
- deepseek: lenticular-bracket + dagger search citations
- grok: grok\_render\_citation\_card\_json
- new perplexity pack: ppl-ai-file-upload, attached\_file,
  utm\_source=perplexity

Also fixes the stale pack list in CLAUDE.md and the store listing copy.
