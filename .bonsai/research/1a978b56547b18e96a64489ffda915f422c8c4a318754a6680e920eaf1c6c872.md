## Summary

Catalog Export

## Compressed

### Catalog Export

Export data on selected products from Business Manager or through a custom pipeline. Export a catalog as an XML or a [CSV].

Business Manager catalog export mode `Export Specific Products` only contains the list of selected products and the category assignments for those products. Click App Launcher _(image: App Launcher)_, and then select ***site* > Merchant Tools > Products and Catalogs > Import & Export > Catalog Export > catalog > product(s)**. You can use the standard product search functionality or the beta search functionality as described in [Simple Products Search].

> **Note:** B2C Commerce Support enables the beta search functionality.

## Detailed

### Catalog Export

Export data on selected products from Business Manager or through a custom pipeline. During catalog export, B2C Commerce automatically trims leading and trailing whitespace and removes any previously saved newline characters in a product image path. Export a catalog as an XML or a [CSV](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=cc.b2c_export_a_catalog_as_csv.htm&type=5).

Business Manager catalog export mode `Export Specific Products` only contains the list of selected products and the category assignments for those products. Click App Launcher _(image: App Launcher)_, and then select ***site* > Merchant Tools > Products and Catalogs > Import & Export > Catalog Export > catalog > product(s)**. You can use the standard product search functionality or the beta search functionality as described in [Simple Products Search](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=cc.b2c_simple_search.htm&type=5).

> **Note:** B2C Commerce Support enables the beta search functionality.
