## Summary

Third-Party Applications > TikTok for Business Integration > Monitor Export Catalog Details

## Compressed

### Monitor Export Catalog Details

Monitor the details of your TikTok export catalog with Manage your TikTok App.

1. In Business Manager, click App Launcher  and then select **Merchant Tools** > **Social Channels** > **TikTok for Business**.

2. Click **Manager your TikTok App**.

   The plugin and integration app is loaded.

   

3. Click **Manage your TikTok App**.

   The TikTok Catalog Manager opens.

4. Review the synced product details.

## Detailed

### Monitor Export Catalog Details

Monitor the details of your TikTok export catalog with Manage your TikTok App.

1. In Business Manager, click App Launcher ![App Launcher](https://a.sfdcstatic.com/developer-website/sfdocs/commerce-cloud/media/shared/appLauncher.png) and then select **Merchant Tools** > **Social Channels** > **TikTok for Business**.

2. Click **Manager your TikTok App**.

   The plugin and integration app is loaded.

   ![Manage your TikTok app.](https://a.sfdcstatic.com/developer-website/sfdocs/commerce-cloud/media/b2c-commerce/b2c_tiktok\_16.png)

3. Click **Manage your TikTok App**.

   The TikTok Catalog Manager opens.

4. Review the synced product details.

   ![TikTok Catalog Manager.](https://a.sfdcstatic.com/developer-website/sfdocs/commerce-cloud/media/b2c-commerce/b2c_tiktok\_17.png)
