# Build Your Consented Marketing Audience at SFRA Checkout

SFRA storefronts now support shopper consent capture at checkout. Capture consents at the moment shoppers place their orders without interrupting order placement.

**Where:** This change applies to B2C Commerce with Agentforce Marketing.

**How:** To show consent checkboxes at checkout, configure a shopper consent for the checkout location in Business Manager. See [Shopper Consent Capture on PWA Kit and SFRA Storefronts]
