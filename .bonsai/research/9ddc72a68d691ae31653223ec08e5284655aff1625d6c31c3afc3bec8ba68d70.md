Figure [3] visualizes the performance in banlist suppression for each method, plotted against output degradation as measured by our writing rubric. The Antislop Sampler achieves perfect suppression (100%) while actually improving writing quality above baseline. FTPO maintains quality within 1% of the baseline performance of gemma-3-12b, while achieving 83-92% suppression rates.

In contrast, DPO and token banning show marked quality degradation. DPO drops 6-15 points in writing quality despite achieving only 80-82% suppression. Token banning collapses even more severely, with quality falling to 28 (out of 100) at 8k patterns.

The writing dataset used for evaluation consists of 1,000 prompts from the same Reddit writing dataset ([Nitral-AI, 2024]) we used in training.
