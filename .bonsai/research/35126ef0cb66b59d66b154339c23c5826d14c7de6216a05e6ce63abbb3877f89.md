## Appendix E DPO β\\beta Hyperparameter Ablation

Figure 8: We examine the impact of DPO’s β\\beta hyperparameter, training gemma-3-12b on our final-token preference set with several values of β\\beta: 0.01, 0.1, 0.3 and 1.0. This training set suppresses a banlist of 1,000 items. With DPO, we observe an expected tradeoff in learnability vs degradation \[[Wu et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.15061#bib.bib33)\]. DPO manages a <1%<1\\% reduction in output quality at β\=1.0\\beta=1.0, but at the expense of significantly impaired banlist suppression (74.7%). At lower values of β\\beta, output quality is markedly reduced for the DPO-trained models. In comparison, the FTPO model trained on the same dataset achieves the highest suppression rate of 94.4% suppression, with neglibible (<1%<1\\%) degradation in longform writing score.
