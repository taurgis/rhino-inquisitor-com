# Work More Efficiently with the Redesigned Business Manager Interface

Access version, updates, and POD details in the Business Manager navigation panel instead of the footer, which has been removed to reduce clutter. Instance and site time zone are available in a tooltip.

**Where:** This change applies to B2C Commerce.
