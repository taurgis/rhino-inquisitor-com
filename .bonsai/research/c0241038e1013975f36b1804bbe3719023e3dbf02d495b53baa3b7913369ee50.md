### Emoji as formatting

*   [WP:AIEMOJI]

AI chatbots have used [emoji] in the past.[\[22\]][\[15\]] In particular, they sometimes decorated section headings or bullet points by placing emoji in front of them. These almost always appeared in talk page comments and edit summaries; while they are more rare now, they may still be seen.
