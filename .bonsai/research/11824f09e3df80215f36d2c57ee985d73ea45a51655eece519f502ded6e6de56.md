## 9 Ethical considerations

In our study, we encourage participants to provide fake details for the LLM to include in draft text. In a real-world setting, users may provide true details to an API-based LLM, introducing privacy risks.

Our study does not consider whether or how participants feel their use of LLMs should be disclosed to their imagined recipient (in this case, that they planned and post-edited the text but did not write the full initial draft themselves). While user perceptions of their ownership of and contributions to the final text may vary ([Reza et al., 2025]; [Hwang et al., 2025], e.g.,), obscuring the model’s contributions via lack of disclosure or via removal of stylistic fingerprints lessens the reader’s ability to identify “who” they are engaging with [Jain et al. (2025)].

In this project, we used GitHub Copilot during implementation and ChatGPT while revising our (human-written) paper drafts.
