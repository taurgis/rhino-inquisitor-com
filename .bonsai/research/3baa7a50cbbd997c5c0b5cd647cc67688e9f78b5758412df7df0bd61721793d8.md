Our analysis reveals several distinct categories of slop. Models fixate on specific character names (“Elara”, “Kael”), sensory clichés (“voice barely above a whisper”), intensifiers (“a profound sense”) and a go-to set of overused descriptives (“unsettlingly”, “shimmered”).
