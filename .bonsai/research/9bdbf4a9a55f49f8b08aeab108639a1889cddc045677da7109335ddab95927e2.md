## Summary

4 Narrative Stance Markers > 4.1 Marker Definitions

## Compressed

### 4.1 Marker Definitions

Eventive clause density

Proportion of clauses containing physical action, motion, or perception verbs (go, walk, run, see, hear, etc.). Higher values indicate more event-driven, situated narration.

First-person eventive density

First-person pronouns immediately followed by action verbs (e.g., “I walked,” “I saw,” “we ran”) per sentence.

Causal connective density

Causal and explanatory connectives (because, therefore, since, as a result) per sentence.

Retrospective framing density

Phrases explicitly marking temporal distance or hindsight (“looking back,” “in retrospect,” “I now realize,” “at the time I didn’t know”) per sentence.

Abstraction density

Abstract nouns (concepts, states, qualities—typically words ending in -tion, -ness, -ity, etc.) per 100 words. Higher values indicate more nominalized and abstract prose.

Two additional markers—concrete noun density and sensory language density—were computed for exploratory purposes.

### 4.1 Marker Definitions

Eventive clause density

Proportion of clauses containing physical action, motion, or perception verbs (go, walk, run, see, hear, etc.). Higher values indicate more event-driven, situated narration.

First-person eventive density

First-person pronouns immediately followed by action verbs (e.g., “I walked,” “I saw,” “we ran”) per sentence. Captures narrator-as-agent constructions often associated with experiential narration.

Causal connective density

Causal and explanatory connectives (because, therefore, since, as a result) per sentence. Indexes explicit causal reasoning in the narrative.

Retrospective framing density

Phrases explicitly marking temporal distance or hindsight (“looking back,” “in retrospect,” “I now realize,” “at the time I didn’t know”) per sentence.

Abstraction density

Abstract nouns (concepts, states, qualities—typically words ending in -tion, -ness, -ity, etc.) per 100 words. Higher values indicate more nominalized and abstract prose.
