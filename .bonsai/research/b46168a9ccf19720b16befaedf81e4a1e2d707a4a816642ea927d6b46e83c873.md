### Boost Conversion with One-Click Checkout

Offer shoppers a streamlined checkout experience and reduce cart abandonment with one-click checkout. When a first-time shopper checks out, they can now save their shipping and payment information for future use and create an account with your store. Their shipping and payment information is automatically filled in.

**Where:** This change applies to Composable Storefronts in B2C Commerce.

**How:** This feature is available for Composable Storefronts after the B2C Commerce 26.3 release and the PWA Kit version 3.17 release. To enable one-click checkout for Composable Storefronts, see [Configure One-Click Checkout for Your B2C Commerce Composable Storefronts].

## Detailed

### Boost Conversion with One-Click Checkout

Offer shoppers a streamlined checkout experience and reduce cart abandonment with one-click checkout. When a first-time shopper checks out, they can now save their shipping and payment information for future use and create an account with your store. When a registered shopper returns to make another purchase, the shopper only needs to enter their email and a verification code to check out. Their shipping and payment information is automatically filled in. Shoppers always have the option to check out as a guest.

**Where:** This change applies to Composable Storefronts in B2C Commerce.

**How:** This feature is available for Composable Storefronts after the B2C Commerce 26.3 release and the PWA Kit version 3.17 release. To enable one-click checkout for Composable Storefronts, see [Configure One-Click Checkout for Your B2C Commerce Composable Storefronts](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/commerce/pwa-kit-managed-runtime/guide/one-click-checkout.html).
