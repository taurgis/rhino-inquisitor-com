# 2027 Certification Retirements FAQ

## 

## Summary

**24 Salesforce certifications will retire on February 1, 2027.** This FAQ covers everything you need to know about the upcoming changes.

*   **Last day to register:** July 24, 2026
    
*   **Last day to take an exam:** August 31, 2026
    
*   **Retirement date:** February 1, 2027

## Certification Retirements FAQs

  
**Q: Which certifications are retiring in 2027?**

A: For more information, review the [retiring certifications list below].
  

**Q: What is the timeline for the certifications’ retirements?**

A: The timeline for the certification retirements is:

*   **Last day to register for an exam:** July 24, 2026  
    
*   **Last day to take an exam:** August 31, 2026
    
*   **Official retirements:** February 1, 2027
    

**Q: Should I pursue a certification exam that is scheduled for retirement?**

A: We allow candidates to continue registering for and taking a retiring exam to provide flexibility and options. If you pass before the retirement deadline **(August 31, 2026)**, you'll earn the certification and it will remain visible on your [Trailblazer Profile]—marked as retired on **February 1, 2027**.

A retired certification is still a valid certification. The retirement of this certification reflects an update to our certification catalog, ensuring its alignment with the latest Salesforce products and in-demand skills.

If you've already registered but want to pursue a different certification instead, you can cancel and receive a refund.

**Q: Why is Salesforce retiring these certifications?**A: The Salesforce Certification program is constantly evolving, which brings changes to our certification exam catalog.
  
Retiring these exams helps us to:
  

*   **Make your certifications more valuable:** We align our certification offerings with the skills and expertise that hiring managers seek in the workplace.
    
*   **Stay current with the product landscape:** As Salesforce technologies evolve, our certification catalog is updated to reflect the skills professionals use daily.
    
*   **Grow your skills where it matters most:** We build certifications around the latest job roles, products, and features to give you the most relevant, in-demand certifications available today.
    

**Q: What are the next steps on my certification journey if my certification will be retiring?**

A: Get details on the path forward for each retiring certification in the [list below]. Note that any retired certifications will remain on your [Trailblazer Profile record] and marked as retired as of February 1, 2027.

**Q: What new exams are currently in development?**

A: We are currently developing new Tableau Next and Marketing Next exams.

**Q: Is a retired certification still valid?**

A retired certification is still a valid certification.
