## Summary

Third-Party Applications > Google Channels Integration > Social Feed Cartridge

## Compressed

### Social Feed Cartridge

Use the Salesforce Commerce Cloud (SFCC) social feeds cartridge for Google Surfaces to publish to Google Surfaces:

- An entire site catalog, or a category of products.
- Information from inventory lists that are imported into B2C Commerce.
- Information from the Omnichannel Inventory(OCI) service.
- List of stores with mapping to inventory lists.
- Price books for products in each store.
