### A.2 Self-Preference on More Datasets

Figure [9] and Figure [10] respectively show the self-preference bias of LLM judges on the TruthfulQA and WMT19 (de-en) datasets. Specifically, both pre-trained and post-trained models exhibit self-preference bias. For instance, when acting as judges, models like Llama-3.1-8B and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct tend to give higher scores to their own responses than gold judgments assign to those responses. Additionally, we observe that large-sized models exhibit less pronounced self-preference bias compared to smaller models. For example, on the TruthfulQA dataset, when large-sized models are paired with small-sized models, the DBG scores of the large-sized models tend to be closer to zero than those of the small-sized models.
