Composable Storefront Release Notes > 5/7/2026 > Change the Order of the Payment Methods for Your Composable Storefront

## Compressed

### Change the Order of the Payment Methods for Your Composable Storefront

Arrange the sequence of payment methods in Business Manager so that shoppers see your preferred options first. Organize Checkout and Express Checkout payment methods for your payment zones. This feature replaces the previous static list.

**Where:** This change applies to Composable Storefronts in B2C Commerce.

**How:** In Business Manager, click App Launcher, and then select **Merchant Tools** | **Site** | **Ordering** | **Salesforce Payments**. Select a payment zone. In the Configured Payment Methods menu, select **Edit**. Drag the Checkout and Express Checkout payment methods into your preferred order.

- [Manage Payment Methods for B2C Commerce]
