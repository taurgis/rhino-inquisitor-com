## Compressed

### 4.2 Evaluation Methodology

We assess each generated report’s adherence to its instructions via deterministic pattern matching. Instructions not found are counted as omission errors. Instructions matching at least an 80%-length prefix of each term are counted as modification errors. We compute per-model and per-density omission and modification rates by aggregating across seeds. To quantify primacy effects, we partition each instruction list into early, middle, and late thirds and measure error rates within each bucket. Standard deviation is computed by taking the sample standard deviation of accuracy scores across the five random seeds at each density level.
