## 6 Perplexity Analysis

If AI rewrites converge toward a common register, they may also become more predictable in distributional terms. Perplexity measures the average uncertainty a language model assigns to a text: lower perplexity indicates that the sequence is more readily predicted by the model, and therefore more typical under that model’s learned distribution. Here, a decrease in perplexity would suggest that rewritten texts have shifted toward more model-typical language.
