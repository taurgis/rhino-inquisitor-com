## Compressed

### Manage Express Payment Visibility Across Your Composable Storefront

Control where Express Checkout payment methods appear on your Composable Storefront using switches in Business Manager. Enable or disable Express Checkout payment methods for the Checkout, Cart, Mini-Cart, and Product Detail pages without modifying code.

**Where:** This change applies to Composable Storefronts in B2C Commerce.

**How:** In Business Manager, click App Launcher, and then select **Merchant Tools** | **Site** | **Ordering** | **Salesforce Payments**. Click **Settings**. In the Express Checkout Display section, select where the Express Checkout payment methods appear on your storefront.
