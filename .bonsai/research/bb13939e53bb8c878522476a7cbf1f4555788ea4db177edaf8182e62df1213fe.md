## Compressed

### Boost International Sales with Expanded Payment Methods for Your Composable Storefront

Provide shoppers with familiar, localized payment methods. Offer Amazon Pay for Stripe and TWINT for Adyen as native Salesforce Payments options.

**Where:** This change applies to Composable Storefronts in B2C Commerce.

**How:** In Business Manager, click App Launcher, and then select **Merchant Tools** | **Site** | **Ordering** | **Salesforce Payments**. Select a payment zone. In the Configured Payment Methods menu, select **Edit** and add a payment method.

- [Manage Payment Methods for B2C Commerce]
