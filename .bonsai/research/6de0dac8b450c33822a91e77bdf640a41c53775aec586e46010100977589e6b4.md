### 4.3 Impact of Post-Training Data on Self-Preference

In this section, we investigate whether fine-tuning two distinct pre-trained models on identical data can help mitigate self-preference bias. Training different models with the same data may encourage the generation of similar responses and align their judgment tendencies.

As shown in Figure [6], fine-tuning different models on the same data helps reduce their self-preference bias.
