## Compressed

### 3.2 Domains

Since errors have been shown to be important clues for detection ([Dugan et al., 2023]), we prioritized domains at high risk for abuse and which were likely to induce errors from language models. Our sources evaluate factual knowledge (News, Wikipedia), generalization and reasoning (Abstracts, Recipes), creative and conversational skills (Reddit, Poetry), and knowledge of specific media (Books, Reviews). For more detailed information on each source domain see Appendix [A.1].
