## Summary

tricolon

## Compressed

Three parallel elements of the same length occurring together in a series.

Example

> Veni, vidi, vici. —Julius Caesar  
> _(__"I came; I saw; I conquered." However, the English is not a true tricolon, for its verbs are not all the same length, as is the case in the Latin)_
