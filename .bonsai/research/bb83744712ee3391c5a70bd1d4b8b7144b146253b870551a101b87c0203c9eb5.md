## Point of Sale FAQ

Salesforce Point of Sale is a modern cloud native, mobile first Point-of-Sale system that brings Salesforce into the store and unifies all digital retail channels. It gives store associates real-time access to inventory, [customer profiles](https://www.salesforce.com/marketing/data/customer-profile/), and order data. This platform bridges the gap between online and in-store shopping to turn locations into flexible [fulfillment](https://www.salesforce.com/commerce/order-management/fulfillment-software/what-is-fulfillment/) centers.

The system enables mobile-first checkout for faster service. Associates can view comprehensive customer profiles to offer personalized help. It provides real-time transparency into product availability. This allows staff to quickly find items or offer [endless aisle](https://www.salesforce.com/retail/endless-aisle/) options for a better shopping experience.

Yes, it supports various [omnichannel](https://www.salesforce.com/commerce/what-is-omnichannel/) fulfillment options like Buy Online, Pick Up In-Store (BOPIS) and Buy Online, Return In-Store (BORIS). Store associates manage these orders directly within the point-of-sale app. This flexibility increases customer convenience and drives foot traffic to physical stores.

Yes, the system handles mixed cart transactions easily. A customer can buy an in-store item and order an out-of-stock item for home delivery in a single transaction. The point-of-sale app processes both the physical purchase and the digital order seamlessly to prevent lost sales.

[Clienteling](https://www.salesforce.com/retail/clienteling/) tools help associates build better customer relationships. Staff can easily sign up new customers at the register or search existing accounts. They can view detailed profiles including online loyalty data and purchase history. This information allows associates to provide highly personalized service.

Yes, the platform includes a reliable offline mode. This feature provides peace of mind during internet outages or network disruptions. Associates can still process transactions and serve customers smoothly. The system will automatically sync the data once the internet connection is restored.

Salesforce Point of Sale supports a wide range of retail hardware. The app runs easily on modern iOS and Android mobile devices. It also integrates seamlessly with standard store equipment. You can connect the system to traditional cash drawers, barcode scanners, receipt printers, and popular payment terminals.

No, you do not need to buy proprietary hardware. The software works with many third-party devices you may already own. It supports receipt printers and standard payment terminals to process transactions securely.

Salesforce acquired PredictSpring to power its modern Point of Sale solution. PredictSpring was a leading cloud-based [retail software](https://www.salesforce.com/retail/operations-software/) vendor. This acquisition brings robust mobile checkout, clienteling, and inventory features directly into the Salesforce ecosystem. It provides retailers with a fully unified platform that connects physical stores with [digital commerce](https://www.salesforce.com/commerce/what-is-digital-commerce/) channels.

Salesforce updated the name of Retail Cloud to Salesforce Point of Sale. This name reflects its unified approach to connecting digital and physical retail channels. The platform continues to offer the same robust features for in-store checkout, [inventory management](https://www.salesforce.com/commerce/order-management/inventory-management-guide/), and omnichannel fulfillment.
