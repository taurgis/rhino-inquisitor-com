## Update: 7 June 2023

I updated [Bug 257458 – AX: Tables with display properties are skipped, report wrong col/row counts] to include results of testing with Safari 16.5 and VoiceOver on macOS 12.6.6:

19.  Tables with `display: flex` on the `<tr>` cannot be navigated into using table navigation.
20.  Tables with `display: flex` on the `<tr>` are skipped when using read-all.
21.  Tables with `display: flex` on the `<tr>` cannot be navigated into using swipe gestures.
22.  Tables with `display: flex` on the `<tr>` are skipped when using read-all.
23.  Tables with `display: block` on the `<td>` and `<th>` give the wrong column count.
24.  Tables with `display: block` on the `<th>` announce each as a cell.
25.  Tables with `display: block` on the `<td>` and `<th>` do not support navigating within a column.
26.  Tables with `display: block` on the `<td>` and `<th>` do not announce column headers.
27.  Tables with `display: inline-block` on the `<td>` and `<th>` give the wrong column count.
28.  Tables with `display: inline-block` on the `<td>` and `<th>` announce the entirety of cells in a row as a single node.
29.  Tables with `display: inline-block` on the `<td>` and `<th>` do not support navigating within a column (because it is treated as one column).
30.  Tables with `display: inline-block` on the `<td>` and `<th>` do not announce column headers.
31.  Tables with `display: contents` on the `<td>` and `<th>` cannot be navigated into using table navigation.
32.  Tables with `display: contents` on the `<td>` and `<th>` are skipped when using read-all.
33.  Tables with `display: contents` on the `<td>` and `<th>` have each row read as a single string of text.

So far I have seen no signals about this from Apple’s [WWDC Safari and CSS talk], despite demonstrating some of these properties.

The [Safari 17 beta announcement] suggests some fixes for `display: contents`, but not for the issues I cite here.
