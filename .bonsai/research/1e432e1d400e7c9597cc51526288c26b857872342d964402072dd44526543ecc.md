## Appendix J Automated Metrics

We report the correlation between automatic text metrics in Figure [11](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19163#A10.F11 "Figure 11 ‣ Appendix J Automated Metrics ‣ Measuring AI “Slop” in Text"). Many metrics have moderate to high correlations indicating shared information.

Figure 11: Correlation news

We also report the distribution of WQRM scores in Figure [12](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19163#A10.F12 "Figure 12 ‣ Appendix J Automated Metrics ‣ Measuring AI “Slop” in Text") split by the (a) News and (b) MS MARCO datasets. The distribution of scores in the News domain is relatively broad. By contrast, the MS MARCO dataset shows a narrower spread, with most scores clustering between 5.5 and 7.0 and fewer documents at the extremes.

(a) News

(b) MS MARCO

Figure 12: Distributions of WQRM scores across the (a) News and (b) MS MARCO datasets.

We train logistic regression models with ℓ\\ell2 regularization using the liblinear solver. Features are standardized with a StandardScaler, and highly correlated features are removed with a threshold of 0.95. Models are tuned over a grid of C∈{0.01,0.1,1,10}C\\in\\{0.01,0.1,1,10\\}. We balance class weights.
