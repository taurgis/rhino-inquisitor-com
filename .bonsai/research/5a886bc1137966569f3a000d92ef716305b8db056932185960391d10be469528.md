#### Writing with InstructGPT reduces lexical diversity.

In [Table 3(a)], we show the diversity measured by nn\-grams (where n\=1,2,3,4,5n=1,2,3,4,5) for essays written in all three groups. We see that the InstructGPT group consistently exhibits lower diversity across all values of nn, whereas GPT3 does not significantly decrease the diversity score.

#### Writing with InstructGPT reduces key point diversity.

In [Table 3(b)], we report the diversity scores measured by the fraction of unique key points. At higher thresholds, the diversity becomes lower for all groups because more key points are considered equivalent. We vary this threshold and find that the InstructGPT group consistently exhibits lower diversity across all selected values.99 9 The same trend holds when using BertScore as a distance measure ([Table 12(b)] in [Appendix C]). In addition, the diversity of the InstructGPT group is lower than both the Solo and GPT3 groups by a statistically significant margin (p<0.05p<0.05) by a pairwise permutation test detailed in [Section B.1].

We verify the significance of the difference in 55\-gram frequencies between InstructGPT and Solo via a chi-square test (p<0.05p<0.05).1111 11 We observe a significant difference in nn\-gram usage for 44\- and 55\-grams as detailed in [Section B.2]. #### Writing with InstructGPT reduces lexical diversity.

In [Table 3(a)](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.05196v3#S5.T3.st1 "In Table 3 ‣ 5.2 Results ‣ 5 Does writing with LLMs reduce the overall diversity? ‣ Does Writing with Language Models Reduce Content Diversity?"), we show the diversity measured by nn\-grams (where n\=1,2,3,4,5n=1,2,3,4,5) for essays written in all three groups. We see that the InstructGPT group consistently exhibits lower diversity across all values of nn, whereas GPT3 does not significantly decrease the diversity score. The reduced lexical diversity showcases a disadvantage of co-writing with LLMs, particularly in creative writing genres like stories and poems, as it may wash away unique expressions on the long tail of human writings.

#### Writing with InstructGPT reduces key point diversity.

In [Table 3(b)](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.05196v3#S5.T3.st2 "In Table 3 ‣ 5.2 Results ‣ 5 Does writing with LLMs reduce the overall diversity? ‣ Does Writing with Language Models Reduce Content Diversity?"), we report the diversity scores measured by the fraction of unique key points. The clustering process involves a threshold that determines the maximum distance above which two clusters will not be merged.88 8 A higher distance threshold indicates that key points having lower similarity can be clustered together. At higher thresholds, the diversity becomes lower for all groups because more key points are considered equivalent. We vary this threshold and find that the InstructGPT group consistently exhibits lower diversity across all selected values.99 9 The same trend holds when using BertScore as a distance measure ([Table 12(b)](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.05196v3#A3.T12.st2 "In Table 12 ‣ Writing with InstructGPT results in more similar content at the key point and raw essay level ‣ Appendix C Additional Results ‣ Does Writing with Language Models Reduce Content Diversity?") in [Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.05196v3#A3 "Appendix C Additional Results ‣ Does Writing with Language Models Reduce Content Diversity?")). More concerning than reduced lexical diversity, this decline in key point diversity raises concerns with long-term social impact, emphasizing the need to safeguard perspectives from underrepresented groups as collaborative writing gains prominence.
