## Compressed

### Streamline Checkout with Address Autocomplete

Reduce shoppers’ keystrokes and errors at the most abandonment-prone step of checkout with address autocomplete. During checkout, a shopper now needs to enter only three characters of their shipping or billing address to instantly receive Google-powered recommendations. When the shopper selects an address from the list of recommendations, Google completes the remaining address fields.

**Where:** This change applies PWA Kit storefronts in B2C Commerce on First Party data centers.

**How:** This feature is available in PWA Kit storefronts starting with the PWA Kit version 3.15 release. To use address autocomplete, see [Configure Address Autocomplete for Your B2C Commerce PWA Kit Storefronts].
