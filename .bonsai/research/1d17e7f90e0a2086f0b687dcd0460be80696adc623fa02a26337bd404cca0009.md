# B2C Commerce Connections

When you use a Salesforce B2C Commerce storefront, you can provision or deactivate integrated features, such as Omnichannel Inventory, Order Self-Service, Order Integration, and Cart and Checkout Data.

*   **[Provision a B2C Commerce Connection]**  
    Set up and activate the B2C Commerce storefront integration or related features.
*   **[Deactivate a B2C Commerce Connection]**  
    Deactivate the B2C Commerce storefront integration or a related feature. Before refreshing or running DBInit on a B2C Commerce instance, deactivate all of its connections.
*   **[Order Self-Service]**  
    Create a customer portal using the SFRA Order Management Self-Service plug-in to let customers manage their orders. Customers can track orders, view order history, and manage cancellations and returns. Order Self-Service supports the RMA return flow, which lets customers return products on their own, but requires that the store validates the return before issuing the refund.
