## 3 IFScale

We propose IFScale, a benchmark designed to investigate how model performance degrades as instruction density increases. The task is to generate a professional business report while including a set of keywords in the output. Each instruction is a constraint to include a specific keyword in the generated report. This allows us to easily scale instruction density from 1010 to 500500 instructions with a step size of 1010 and automatically grade performance by keyword inclusion.
