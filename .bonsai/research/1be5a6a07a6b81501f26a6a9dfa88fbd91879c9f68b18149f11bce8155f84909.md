## Reproducibility Statement

To ensure reproducibility, we provide details of our user study in [Section A.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18161#A1.SS1 "A.1 User Study Recruitment Process, Compensation and Duration ‣ Appendix A User Study Details ‣ How LLMs Distort Our Written Language"), containing the instructions provided to participants who were tasked to write without an LLM, as well as the pre-study and post-study questions provided. We also provide the prompts used for our analysis of the ArgRewrite-v2 dataset in [Section B.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18161#A2.SS1 "B.1 Generating LLM Drafts ‣ Appendix B ArgRewrite-v2 Analysis ‣ How LLMs Distort Our Written Language"), as well as full results on the semantic ([Section B.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18161#A2.SS3 "B.3 Semantic Shifts with MiniLM-L6-v2 Embedding ‣ Appendix B ArgRewrite-v2 Analysis ‣ How LLMs Distort Our Written Language"), [Section B.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18161#A2.SS2 "B.2 Semantic Shifts with Gemini-004 Embedding ‣ Appendix B ArgRewrite-v2 Analysis ‣ How LLMs Distort Our Written Language"), and [Section B.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18161#A2.SS4 "B.4 Semantic Shifts Across Settings / Model ‣ Appendix B ArgRewrite-v2 Analysis ‣ How LLMs Distort Our Written Language")), lexical ([Section B.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18161#A2.SS5 "B.5 Jensen-Shannon Divergence ‣ Appendix B ArgRewrite-v2 Analysis ‣ How LLMs Distort Our Written Language")), emotional ([Section B.7](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18161#A2.SS7 "B.7 Emotional Shift ‣ Appendix B ArgRewrite-v2 Analysis ‣ How LLMs Distort Our Written Language")), and parts of speech ([Section B.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18161#A2.SS6 "B.6 POS Distribution ‣ Appendix B ArgRewrite-v2 Analysis ‣ How LLMs Distort Our Written Language")) analyses, and for robustness, perform quantitative experiments across models and various prompts for editing grounded in our human-user study. Lastly, we provide analysis on several other ICLR categories, and provide the prompts used for the analysis in [Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18161#A3 "Appendix C ICLR Review Analyses ‣ How LLMs Distort Our Written Language").
