## Summary

Composable Storefront Release Notes > 9/25/2025 > Enable Agentforce Guided Shopping

## Compressed

### Enable Agentforce Guided Shopping

Enable the Guided Shopping Agent for B2C Storefronts to provide an AI-assisted shopping experience in your storefront. Visitors to your storefront can take advantage of the agent's assistance to discover and purchase products to improve the shopping experience and help boost sales.

#### Required Licenses

Before you turn on the Guided Shopping Agent, enable these required licenses and required add-on licenses:

Required Licenses

- B2C Commerce
- Agentforce Service or Agentforce Sales in Enterprise or Unlimited editions

Required Add-on Licenses

- Agentforce Add-on
- Supported Editions for Messaging

**See Also**

- *Salesforce Help*: [Agentforce for Guided Shopping for B2C Commerce]

Enable the Guided Shopping Agent for B2C Storefronts to provide an AI-assisted shopping experience in your storefront. This agent is powered by generative AI and advanced language models.
