## Summary

B2C Commerce API Release Notes > 09/23/2026 > SLAS Release

## Compressed

### SLAS Release

- SLAS Infrastructure Deployment:
  - During the deployment of required infrastructure updates, registered shopper logins and token refresh calls may have elevated response times and 5xx errors for less than one minute. Our internal simulations of production peak traffic resulted in less than 15 seconds of performance degradation, with no impact on existing sessions and guest shoppers.
  - The deployment is scheduled at these times (UTC):
    - AP2: September 23, 16:00
    - AP3: September 23, 17:00
    - EU-Central: September 23, 23:00
    - US-East: September 24, 03:00
