## Appendix A Unassigned and unobserved lines

This appendix presents tables of lines present in the 10 Leo spectrum but not assigned to an element or molecule yet (Tables [14](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.12467#A1.T14 "Table 14 ‣ Appendix A Unassigned and unobserved lines ‣ CRIRES-POP: a library of high resolution spectra in the near-infrared. III. Line identification in the K-giant 10 Leo") and [15](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.12467#A1.T15 "Table 15 ‣ Appendix A Unassigned and unobserved lines ‣ CRIRES-POP: a library of high resolution spectra in the near-infrared. III. Line identification in the K-giant 10 Leo")). In addition, we give tables of lines expected to be visible from the VALD3 database, but not observed in the spectra of 10 Leo or Arcturus (Tables [16](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.12467#A1.T16 "Table 16 ‣ Appendix A Unassigned and unobserved lines ‣ CRIRES-POP: a library of high resolution spectra in the near-infrared. III. Line identification in the K-giant 10 Leo") to [18](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.12467#A1.T18 "Table 18 ‣ Appendix A Unassigned and unobserved lines ‣ CRIRES-POP: a library of high resolution spectra in the near-infrared. III. Line identification in the K-giant 10 Leo")). For the YJ band, the corresponding tables can be found in the main part of the paper (Tables [7](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.12467#S2.T7 "Table 7 ‣ 2.4 Unidentified lines ‣ 2 Spectral lines ‣ CRIRES-POP: a library of high resolution spectra in the near-infrared. III. Line identification in the K-giant 10 Leo") and [8](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.12467#S2.T8 "Table 8 ‣ 2.5 Incorrect transitions in VALD3 ‣ 2 Spectral lines ‣ CRIRES-POP: a library of high resolution spectra in the near-infrared. III. Line identification in the K-giant 10 Leo")).
