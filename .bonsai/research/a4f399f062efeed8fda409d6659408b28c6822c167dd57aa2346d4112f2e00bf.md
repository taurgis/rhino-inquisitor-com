### 3.3 Selected models

We rely on six pre-trained generative language models that are representative within the NLP community. These models cover 4 different sizes (7, 13, 30 and 65 billion parameters) and 3 model families. We only include different sizes for LLaMa as results within the same family are similar, and larger models need considerably more compute. We briefly mention their main particularities below:

#### 3.3.1 LLaMa models (LL) (Touvron et al. LLaMa is based on the Transformer architecture and integrates several innovations from other large language models. In comparison to larger models like GPT-3 (Brown et al. #### 3.3.2 Falcon 7B (F7B) (Almazrouei et al. [2023](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-024-10903-2#ref-CR3 "Almazrouei E, Alobeidli H, Alshamsi A, Cappelli A, Cojocaru R, Debbah M, Goffinet É, Hesslow D, Launay J, Malartic Q et al (2023) The falcon series of open language models. arXiv preprint 
arXiv:2311.16867
"))

Introduced alongside its larger variants with 40 and 180 billion parameters, Falcon 1 7B is trained on 1.5 trillion tokens from a mix of curated and web datasets (see Table [1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-024-10903-2#Tab1)). Its architecture relies on multigroup attention (an advanced form of multiquery attention), Rotary Embeddings (similar to LLaMa), standard GeLU activation, parallel attention, MLP blocks, and omits biases in linear layers. We primarily chose this model to compare the results in the following sections with those of its counterpart, LLaMa 7B, and to explore whether there are significant differences among models of similar size.

#### 3.3.3 Mistral 7B (M7B) (Jiang et al. [2023](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-024-10903-2#ref-CR31 "Jiang AQ, Sablayrolles A, Mensch A, Bamford C, Chaplot DS, Casas Ddl, Bressand F, Lengyel G, Lample G, Saulnier L et al (2023) Mistral 7b. arXiv preprint 
arXiv:2310.06825
"))

Mistral v0.1 surpasses larger LLaMa models in various benchmarks despite its smaller size. Its distinctive architecture features Sliding Window Attention, Rolling Buffer Cache, and Prefill and Chunking. The training data for Mistral 7B is not publicly disclosed, and to fight against data contamination issues, our analysis only includes articles published after the model’s release. The choice of this model as an object of study follows the same thinking we used for the Falcon model. We want to see how well Mistral 7B does and how its new features stack up against models of the same size.
