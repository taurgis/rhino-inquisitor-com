## Summary

Configure Multisite URLs > Switch Sites, Locales, and Currencies > Site Switcher

## Compressed

### Site Switcher

The site switcher component is at `src/components/site-switcher`. When a shopper selects a new site:

1. The dropdown calls `i18n.changeLanguage` with the default locale of the new site.
2. A `POST` is submitted to `/action/set-site-context` with the new `siteId` and `localeId`.
3. The server action sets the `site_id` and `lng` cookies and issues a redirect.
4. The page reloads with the new site and locale applied.
