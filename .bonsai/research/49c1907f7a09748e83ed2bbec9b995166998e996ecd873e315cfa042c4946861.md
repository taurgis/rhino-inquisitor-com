## Ethics Statement

We adhere to the ICLR Code of Ethics ([https://iclr.cc/public/CodeOfEthics]). We processed only public text and did not collect or annotate human subjects. No personally identifying information was collected, and no IRB was required.

Potential harms include: (i) unintended suppression of legitimate dialects, or minority styles; (ii) attempts to evade AI-text detection. Mitigations: our code produces human-readable banlists which may be vetted by hand before deployment; we document and expose the _ban-strength_ control (Sec. [4.2]) and provide soft-ban defaults rather than hard blocking; we implement a whitelist to prevent terms from being automatically banned; we recommend human review of any production banlist. Our methods do not target model safety filters and are not intended to bypass them.

We transparently report throughput impacts (App. [B] ‣ Antislop: A Comprehensive Framework for Identifying and Eliminating Repetitive Patterns in Language Models")) to support energy-cost accounting.

## Ethics Statement

We adhere to the ICLR Code of Ethics ([https://iclr.cc/public/CodeOfEthics](https://iclr.cc/public/CodeOfEthics)). Our study operates on publicly available datasets and benchmarks: Reddit SFW Writing Prompts via Nitral-AI ([Nitral-AI, 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.15061#bib.bib17)), EQ-Bench creative prompts ([Paech, 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.15061#bib.bib19)), Project Gutenberg texts ([Project Gutenberg,](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.15061#bib.bib21) ), and wordfreq statistics ([Speer et al., 2018](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.15061#bib.bib26)). We processed only public text and did not collect or annotate human subjects. No personally identifying information was collected, and no IRB was required.

Potential harms include: (i) unintended suppression of legitimate dialects, or minority styles; (ii) attempts to evade AI-text detection. Mitigations: our code produces human-readable banlists which may be vetted by hand before deployment; we document and expose the _ban-strength_ control (Sec. [4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.15061#S4.SS2 "4.2 Soft Banning: Configurable Suppression Strength ‣ 4 The Antislop Sampler ‣ Antislop: A Comprehensive Framework for Identifying and Eliminating Repetitive Patterns in Language Models")) and provide soft-ban defaults rather than hard blocking; we implement a whitelist to prevent terms from being automatically banned; we recommend human review of any production banlist. Our methods do not target model safety filters and are not intended to bypass them.

We transparently report throughput impacts (App. [B](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.15061#A2 "Appendix B Inference Performance (tok/s) ‣ Antislop: A Comprehensive Framework for Identifying and Eliminating Repetitive Patterns in Language Models")) to support energy-cost accounting. The authors declare no conflicts of interest, no external sponsorship that biases results, and disclose LLM assistance for drafting as stated in the paper’s AI Usage Disclosure.
