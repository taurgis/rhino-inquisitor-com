### 3.4 Attractor strength and position

Human cultural evolution shows that cultural traits sometimes evolve towards attractor states, i.e., content that invites convergence even with different starting points ([Kalish et al., 2007]; [Miton et al., 2015]; [Miton et al., 2020]; [Buskell, 2017]). We mathematically define attractors in terms of two properties of interest: its position (i.e., the location in output generation space the process converges toward) and its strength (i.e., the intensity to which generated outputs are pulled toward it). Therefore, we were interested in whether transmission chains with LLMs would show similar attractor dynamics, and whether these depend on the model and task used in the chain.
