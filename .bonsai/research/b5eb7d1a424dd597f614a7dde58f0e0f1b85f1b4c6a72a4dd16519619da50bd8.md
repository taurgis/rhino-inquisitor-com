## Update: 20 September 2022

Following some [informal community polling](https://github.com/Fyrd/caniuse/pull/6451#issuecomment-1251843371), Can I Use has restored Safari’s “partial support” entry for `display: contents`:

![The Can I Use table for display contents showing Safari 16, 16.1, and TP all only partially support the feature.](/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/caniuse_display-contents.png)

[CSS display: contents](https://caniuse.com/css-display-contents) on Can I Use

My efforts have probably earned me no fans at Apple, though at least [one Apple devrel person might agree](https://twitter.com/jensimmons/status/1572251622058622976) with the change.
