## Compressed

### 5.1 Limitations of Direct Preference Optimization

Direct Preference Optimization’s (DPO) ([Rafailov et al., 2023]) primary hyperparameter for constraining updates (β\\beta) is a coarse tool, impairing learning at high values and causing model degradation by allowing large logit divergences from reference at small (β\\beta) ([Wu et al., 2024]).

Like FTPO, DPO can train on final-token pairs to suppress slop. However, DPO updates only one chosen token per sample, whereas FTPO updates multiple preferred tokens simultaneously.
