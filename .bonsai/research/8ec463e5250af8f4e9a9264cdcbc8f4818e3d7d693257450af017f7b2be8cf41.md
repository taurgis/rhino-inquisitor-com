# Group Pages and Modules into Sections to Improve Navigation

Tailor your workspace by grouping pages and modules into custom sections. Create, rename, and remove sections to build a navigation structure that matches your workflow.

**Where:** This change applies to B2C Commerce.

**How:** To add a section, click **Create Section** in the left navigation. You can also rename or delete a section.. Deleting a section removes all its modules from the navigation.
