## Compressed

### AI detection tools

*   [WP:AIDETECTION]
*   [WP:AIDETECTOR]

Do not solely rely on [artificial intelligence content detection] tools (such as [GPTZero] and [Pangram] "Pangram (AI detector)")). While they perform better than random chance, these tools have non-trivial error rates.[\[1\]][\[2\]] Detectors can be susceptible to factors such as text modifications (e.g. paraphrasing, markup, and spacing changes) and the use of models not seen during detector training.[\[3\]] A high percentage of detected AI writing from the results of a detection tool is **not** a valid criterion for speedy deletion under G15.
