## Summary

Set up the cartridge > Step 3: Generate an API key and client key > Generate a client key in your Customer Area

## Compressed

### Generate a client key in your Customer Area

1. Log in to your [Customer Area].
2. Go to **Developers** > **API credentials**, and select the credential username for your integration, for example **ws\@Company.\[YourCompanyAccount]**.
3. Under **Client settings** > **Authentication** select the **Client key** tab.
4. Select **Generate client key**.
5. Select the copy icon **and store your client key securely in your system.
6. Under **Add allowed origins**, enter your [domains] and select **Add**.
7. Select **Save changes**.

After generating both keys, add them to your Salesforce Business Manager:

1. In your Salesforce Business Manager, go to **Merchant Tools** > **Site Preferences** > **Adyen settings** > **Account settings**.
2. Under **Adyen Client Key**, enter the [client key that you generated].
3. Under **API key of webservice**, enter the [API key that you generated].
4. Select **Save Changes**.
