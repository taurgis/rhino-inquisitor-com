## 3\. Methodology

We conducted a controlled experiment with 118118 participants, comprising 6060 Indian and 5858 American users, recruited through the online crowdsourcing platform Prolific. Participants completed short writing tasks designed to elicit cultural values and artifacts. We had a standard 2×22\\times 2 study design in which participants from India and the US completed the tasks with or without AI suggestions. Subsequently, we compared the essays written by participants from the four groups. Below, we describe our methodology in detail.
