## Update: 16 September 2024

I have cautioned for some time now, including the prior update, that you must test every use of `display: contents` and not rely solely on my limited tested in this post (and prior posts) nor claims of browsers.

As evidence:

> Fixed `display: contents` on `tbody` elements preventing table rows from being properly exposed in the accessibility tree. ([282413@main]) (129131780)

In other words, tables are still broken in Safari if you use `display: contents`, and will be _at least_ until this latest fix makes it to a production Safari release. I have no interest in going through every permutation of every table construct to test other things Apple has missed. That’s Apple’s job.
