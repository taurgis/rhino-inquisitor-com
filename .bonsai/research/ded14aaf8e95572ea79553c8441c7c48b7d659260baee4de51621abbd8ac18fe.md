## Detailed

### 3.4 Models

We carefully chose a set of models that were maximally distinct from each other, offering us the widest range and variability of generated text. We focused on varying model sizes, open/closed source, and free/paid usage. Following work by [Sarvazyan et al. (2023a)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07940v1#bib.bib70), we selected the largest model from each model family. Most importantly, we excluded third-party fine-tuned variants of base models in favor of the base models themselves. We included the official chat versions when available to isolate the effects of chat fine-tuning. In total, we used four GPT models (GPT2, GPT3, GPT4, ChatGPT), three open-source models and their chat variants (Mistral 7B, MPT 30B, LLaMA 2 70B), and the Cohere command and chat model.
