### A.5 Few-shot Setting Analysis

To guide pre-trained models in making judgments, we leverage their few-shot learning ability and prepend examples to each input. For post-trained models, due to their strong instruction-following ability, we prompt them to make judgments in a zero-shot setting. To investigate the differences in judgment between zero-shot and few-shot settings for post-trained models, we conduct judgment experiments under the few-shot setting.
