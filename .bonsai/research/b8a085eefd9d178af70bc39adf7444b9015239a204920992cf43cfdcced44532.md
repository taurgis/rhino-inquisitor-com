## Summary

Page Designer > Use Salesforce CMS Content with Page Designer > Connect Salesforce CMS to Page Designer

## Compressed

### Connect Salesforce CMS to Page Designer

Connect your B2C Commerce instance to a CMS channel and assign the channel to your site library.

1. Open a support ticket and request a trust relationship between your Salesforce org and your B2C Commerce instance.

2. In Salesforce CMS, add a CMS workspace to host the Page Designer content or use an existing CMS workspace.

   For information about creating CMS workspaces, see [Create a CMS Workspace in Salesforce CMS].

   

3. In Salesforce CMS, create a B2C Commerce channel.

   For information about creating a CMS channel, see [Create a CMS Channel].

   

4. In the CMS workspace, add the B2C Commerce channel.

   For information about adding CMS channels, see [Add a Channel to a CMS Workspace].

   

5. In B2C Commerce Business Manager, go to **Administration** > **Sites** > **Content Libraries** > ***site*** > **General**. Scroll to the bottom and assign the CMS channel to your site library.

### Connect Salesforce CMS to Page Designer

Connect your B2C Commerce instance to a CMS channel and assign the channel to your site library.

1. Open a support ticket and request a trust relationship between your Salesforce org and your B2C Commerce instance.

2. In Salesforce CMS, add a CMS workspace to host the Page Designer content or use an existing CMS workspace.

   For information about creating CMS workspaces, see [Create a CMS Workspace in Salesforce CMS](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=xcloud.cms_cmsworkspace-create.htm).

   ![Add CMS workspace](https://a.sfdcstatic.com/developer-website/sfdocs/commerce-cloud/media/b2c-commerce/b2c_page_designer_create_cms_workspace.jpg)

3. In Salesforce CMS, create a B2C Commerce channel.

   For information about creating a CMS channel, see [Create a CMS Channel](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=xcloud.cms_channel_create.htm).

   ![Create CMS channel](https://a.sfdcstatic.com/developer-website/sfdocs/commerce-cloud/media/b2c-commerce/b2c_page_designer_create_cms_channel.jpg)

4. In the CMS workspace, add the B2C Commerce channel.

   For information about adding CMS channels, see [Add a Channel to a CMS Workspace](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=xcloud.cms_channel_add.htm).

   ![Add channel](https://a.sfdcstatic.com/developer-website/sfdocs/commerce-cloud/media/b2c-commerce/b2c_page_designer_add_cms_channel.jpg)

5. In B2C Commerce Business Manager, go to **Administration** > **Sites** > **Content Libraries** > ***site*** > **General**. Scroll to the bottom and assign the CMS channel to your site library.

   ![Assign channel to site library](https://a.sfdcstatic.com/developer-website/sfdocs/commerce-cloud/media/b2c-commerce/b2c_page_designer_cms_channel_selection.jpg)
