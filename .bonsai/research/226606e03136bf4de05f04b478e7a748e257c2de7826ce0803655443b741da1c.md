## Summary

Tells Appear and Disappear Between Model Versions > Gemini

## Compressed

### Gemini

Across Gemini versions, contractions nearly disappear, while intensifiers and precision language become more common.

Source: Graphite research, AI Tells (2026). The analysis uses 10,000 human articles and 90,000 AI-generated articles from nine models on the same topics. Contractions include “it's,” “don't,” “you're,” “isn't,” “doesn't,” “wasn't,” “you'll,” “we've,” and “let's.” Intensifiers include “absolutely,” “entirely,” “highly,” and “incredibly.” Precision language includes “exactly,” “precisely,” and “specifically.”

## Detailed

### Gemini

Across Gemini versions, contractions nearly disappear, while intensifiers and precision language become more common.

Source: Graphite research, AI Tells (2026). The analysis uses 10,000 human articles and 90,000 AI-generated articles from nine models on the same topics. Values show length-normalized usage relative to human writing (1x). Contractions include “it's,” “don't,” “you're,” “isn't,” “doesn't,” “wasn't,” “you'll,” “we've,” and “let's.” Intensifiers include “absolutely,” “entirely,” “highly,” and “incredibly.” Precision language includes “exactly,” “precisely,” and “specifically.”

Anthropic calls writing that substitutes metaphor and flourish for direct statement [mannered prose](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-fable-5-1). It includes phrases like “a dial worth turning” where “a parameter worth varying” would be more direct. Claude Opus 5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro have mannered-prose scores about 2.5 times the human score, while GPT-6 Astra’s mannered-prose score is much closer to the human score.

Source: Graphite research, AI Tells (2026). The analysis uses 10,000 human articles and 90,000 AI-generated articles from nine models on the same topics. Values show the mean mannered-prose score relative to human writing (1x) across a subsample of 1,000 topics. We use Claude Opus 5 to score each article’s mannered prose from 0 to 100 using Anthropic’s definition.

Changes in em-dash use vary by model family. Em-dash use falls sharply from GPT-5 to GPT-5.6 Sol and remains low in Astra. It nearly disappears between the two Gemini versions. Claude Opus 4.6 uses fewer em dashes than Opus 4, but Opus 5 brings them back to about the human rate. GPT and Gemini may have overcorrected for this well-known tell. Their latest versions use em dashes far less often than human writers.

Source: Graphite research, AI Tells (2026). The analysis uses 10,000 human articles and 90,000 AI-generated articles from nine models on the same topics. Values show mean em-dash counts per 1,000 words relative to the human mean (1×).
