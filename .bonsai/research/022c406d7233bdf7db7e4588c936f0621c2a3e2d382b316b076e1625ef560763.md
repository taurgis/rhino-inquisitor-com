## Summary

Set up the cartridge > Step 9: Use risk management features > Data collection

## Compressed

### Data collection

The cartridge [collects risk data] on the checkout page and sends it to Adyen by default.

In addition to the data that you send when the shopper checks out, you can also optionally collect data to on any of your [Salesforce ISML templates] that generate your storefront pages to detect fraud outside of your checkout page.

Refer to [Collect additional data outside of the checkout page] to set up data collection on any of your web pages.

## Detailed

### Data collection

The cartridge [collects risk data](/risk-management/fraud-data-collection/#data-collection-components) on the checkout page and sends it to Adyen by default.

In addition to the data that you send when the shopper checks out, you can also optionally collect data to on any of your [Salesforce ISML templates](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/commerce/b2c-commerce/guide/b2c-working-with-templates.html) that generate your storefront pages to detect fraud outside of your checkout page.

Refer to [Collect additional data outside of the checkout page](/risk-management/fraud-data-collection/#collect-additional-data) to set up data collection on any of your web pages.
