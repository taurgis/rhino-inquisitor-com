## Summary

Page Designer Integration with Storefront Next > Business Manager Setup > Shopper Context Preview

## Compressed

### Shopper Context Preview

To preview a page in the context of a specific Customer Group or on a specific date:

1. **Enable the feature toggle in Business Manager**: Turn on the "Shopper Context" feature toggle in Business Manager.
2. **Configure MRT environment variable**: Set `PUBLIC__app__features__shopperContext__enabled` to `true` in your Managed Runtime environment.
