## Summary

Set up the cartridge > Step 9: Use risk management features > Basket fields

## Compressed

### Basket fields

You can create [custom risk rules] to influence the risk score of a transaction. Custom risk rules are a [RevenueProtect] premium feature.

You can use Basket fields for [custom risk rules]. To include the Basket fields in your payment request:

1. In the Business Manager, go to **Merchant tools** > **Site Preferences** > **Adyen Settings** > **Additional Settings**.
2. Under **Basket fields**, select **Enable**.
3. Select **Save Changes**.
