## 2 Related Work

This article sits at the intersection of digital humanities work on textual mediation, research on stylistic evidence in narrative analysis, and emerging scholarship on AI-assisted writing. While scholars have shown that texts are shaped by editorial, archival, and computational interventions, less is known about whether LLM-based revision systematically alters the linguistic features that humanists later treat as evidence of voice, perspective, and narrative form.
