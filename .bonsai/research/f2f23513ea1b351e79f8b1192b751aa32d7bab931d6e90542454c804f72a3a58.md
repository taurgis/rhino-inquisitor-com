## Compressed

### Search and Page Through Your Project List

Find any project in your organization with the new project search box in Runtime Admin. The search filters across all your projects, not just the ones on the current page. You can also choose how many projects to show per page (20, 50, or 100). Your search term and page size are saved in the page URL, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.

**Where:** This change applies to Managed Runtime.
