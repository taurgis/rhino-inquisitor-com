### 3.2 Pilot “Slop” Annotation

As an initial validation of the taxonomy, we hire 5 professional copy-editors from the Upwork platform44 4 [https://www.upwork.com/] to annotate “slop” spans in two datasets: News article generation ([Russell et al., 2025]); and Retrieval Augmented QA (MS MARCO; [Bajaj et al., 2018]).55 5 Exempt Determination obtained from our institutional IRB. The MS MARCO dataset took ∼\\sim4-7 minutes to annotate, with an average of 55 words per passage. We provided annotators with a guide containing codes for indicators of “slop” from our expert definition survey (§[3]; Appendix Table [7]). We asked annotators to read the text in full, and first answer whether they initially perceive the text as “slop.” We then had annotators label spans in the text (word-level) that instantiate any of the “slop” codes. We provide the full set of questions given to annotators in Appendix Figure [6].66 6 We built the labelling interface using a custom template in LabelStudio: [https://labelstud.io/].

Annotation comprising multi-label, multi-span labelling is a difficult task and requires collaborative stages of task training and alignment among annotators. After independently completing the pilot round, annotators met to review codes and annotation strategy. Here, the guide was discussed in detail: clarifications around labelling strategy (e.g., whether to select only the most salient codes versus coding every feature), and terminology (e.g., Fluency as a measure of language naturalness versus correct grammar) were adjudicated.

### 3.2 Pilot “Slop” Annotation

As an initial validation of the taxonomy, we hire 5 professional copy-editors from the Upwork platform44 4 [https://www.upwork.com/](https://www.upwork.com/) to annotate “slop” spans in two datasets: News article generation ([Russell et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19163#bib.bib14)); and Retrieval Augmented QA (MS MARCO; [Bajaj et al., 2018](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19163#bib.bib6)).55 5 Exempt Determination obtained from our institutional IRB. See Ethical Considerations for approval details. These datasets span different writing styles, expected passage lengths, and serve different purposes for the reader. We paid annotators at a rate of $35-45 USD an hour. Each article took ∼\\sim10-15 minutes to annotate, with an average of 871 words per article. The MS MARCO dataset took ∼\\sim4-7 minutes to annotate, with an average of 55 words per passage. We provided annotators with a guide containing codes for indicators of “slop” from our expert definition survey (§[3](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19163#S3 "3 Defining LLM “Slop” ‣ Measuring AI “Slop” in Text"); Appendix Table [7](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19163#A6.F7 "Figure 7 ‣ Appendix F Annotation Details ‣ Measuring AI “Slop” in Text")). We asked annotators to read the text in full, and first answer whether they initially perceive the text as “slop.” We then had annotators label spans in the text (word-level) that instantiate any of the “slop” codes. Texts may have span annotations even if not initially deemed by the annotator to be “slop” overall. We provide the full set of questions given to annotators in Appendix Figure [6](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19163#A6.F6 "Figure 6 ‣ Appendix F Annotation Details ‣ Measuring AI “Slop” in Text").66 6 We built the labelling interface using a custom template in LabelStudio: [https://labelstud.io/](https://labelstud.io/). In sum, each annotator annotated the same 10 articles and 10 passages in the pilot round.

Annotation comprising multi-label, multi-span labelling is a difficult task and requires collaborative stages of task training and alignment among annotators. After independently completing the pilot round, annotators met to review codes and annotation strategy. Here, the guide was discussed in detail: clarifications around labelling strategy (e.g., whether to select only the most salient codes versus coding every feature), and terminology (e.g., Fluency as a measure of language naturalness versus correct grammar) were adjudicated. Most disagreements came from labelling strategy and terminology, rather than disagreements over the text spans. Table [1](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19163#S3.T1 "Table 1 ‣ 3 Defining LLM “Slop” ‣ Measuring AI “Slop” in Text") shows the final themes and codes after annotator discussion.
