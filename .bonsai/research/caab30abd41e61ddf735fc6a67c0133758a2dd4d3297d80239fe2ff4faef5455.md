### 5.3 Probability-Based Feature Classifiers

Probability-based Feature Classifiers are based on the assumption that text generated by an LLM is generally more likely under the model’s probability distribution than similar text written by a human. GLTR ([Gehrmann et al., 2019]) uses entropy, probability, and probability rank to determine whether or not the statistical patterns in the text are AI-generated. DetectGPT ([Mitchell et al., 2023]) uses perturbations made by a T5 model to compare the probability of a source text to perturbed texts, finding that source texts with a high probability discrepancy against their perturbations are likely to be LLM generated. DetectGPT is a white-box method, meaning that the original LLM used to generate the text is known. Sniffer ([Li et al., 2023]) uses perplexity-based features to detect AI-generated text and also which LLM produced it.

## Detailed

### 5.3 Probability-Based Feature Classifiers

Probability-based Feature Classifiers are based on the assumption that text generated by an LLM is generally more likely under the model’s probability distribution than similar text written by a human. GLTR ([Gehrmann et al., 2019](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib34)) uses entropy, probability, and probability rank to determine whether or not the statistical patterns in the text are AI-generated. The original version of GPTZero ([Tian and Cui, 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib5)) was based on both perplexity and burstiness: the variation of perplexity across the token sequence. DetectGPT ([Mitchell et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib9)) uses perturbations made by a T5 model to compare the probability of a source text to perturbed texts, finding that source texts with a high probability discrepancy against their perturbations are likely to be LLM generated. DetectGPT is a white-box method, meaning that the original LLM used to generate the text is known. However, it was recently shown that smaller language models can be used as proxy models for DetectGPT with reasonable accuracy ([Mireshghallah et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib16)). DetectLLM ([Su et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib35)) builds upon DetectGPT by also introducing similar methods based on log-rank information. Sniffer ([Li et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14873v3#bib.bib36)) uses perplexity-based features to detect AI-generated text and also which LLM produced it.
