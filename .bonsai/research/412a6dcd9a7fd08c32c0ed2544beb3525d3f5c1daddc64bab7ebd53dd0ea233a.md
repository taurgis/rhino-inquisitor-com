### Promotional and advertisement-like language

*   [WP:AIPUFFERY]
*   [WP:AIPEACOCK]

|  | Words to watch: boasts a, vibrant, rich, profound, enhancing, showcasing, exemplifies, commitment to, natural beauty, nestled, in the heart of, groundbreaking, renowned, featuring, diverse array, ... |
| --- | --- |

LLMs have serious problems keeping a neutral tone. Even when prompted to use an encyclopedic style, their output will often tend toward advertisement-like writing, or like the prose of a travel guide.

Note: Not all promotional or spammy writing is AI-generated. LLMs tend to over-use the same set of promotional phrases no matter what the topic. Also, older LLMs (e.g., GPT-4) tend to output more blatantly positive text[\[14\]] than newer LLMs, which are more subtly positive and tend to avoid obviously superlative statements like "the best."

#### Subtypes

When writing about something that could be considered "cultural heritage," LLMs [constantly remind the reader of its importance].

From its scenic landscapes to its historical landmarks, Alamata Raya Kobo offers visitors a fascinating glimpse into the diverse tapestry of Ethiopia. In this article, we will explore the unique characteristics that make Alamata Raya Kobo a town worth visiting and shed light on its significance within the Amhara region.— From this June 2023 revision to Alamata (woreda) |

LLMs have serious problems keeping a neutral tone. Even when prompted to use an encyclopedic style, their output will often tend toward advertisement-like writing, or like the prose of a travel guide. This may happen when generating new text or rewriting existing text: for instance, an edit summary claiming a rewrite "removed promotional tone" while actually introducing it. This may also happen when editors are not [deliberately trying to advertise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:COI "Wikipedia:COI") a subject.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing#cite_note-rettberg-16)

Note: Not all promotional or spammy writing is AI-generated. LLMs tend to over-use the same set of promotional phrases no matter what the topic. Also, older LLMs (e.g., GPT-4) tend to output more blatantly positive text[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing#cite_note-sussman-17) than newer LLMs, which are more subtly positive and tend to avoid obviously superlative statements like "the best."

When writing about something that could be considered "cultural heritage," LLMs [constantly remind the reader of its importance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing#Undue_emphasis_on_significance,_legacy,_and_broader_trends).
