## 7 Ethics and Responsible Use

Our classifier is available for public commercial use at https://pangram.com. We strongly discourage the use of our classifier as a sole arbiter of academic integrity and plagiarism checking. All AI detection tools have a nonzero false positive rate, and should be used in conjunction with other evidence to prove or disprove plagiarism.

AI detection is not a substitute nor a reliable tool for proving the factuality or verity of textual information such as news and media. While AI is often used for disinformation, scams, or other malicious activities, human-authored text can also be used with malintent. One should always check the sources of any text in publications for truth independently.
