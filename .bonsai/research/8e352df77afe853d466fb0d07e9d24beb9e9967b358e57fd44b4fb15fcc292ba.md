### Account structure

With Adyen, you have a single [company account], and one or more sub-accounts called [merchant accounts]. However, in Salesforce, you can only have one merchant account for each storefront.

## Detailed

### Account structure

With Adyen, you have a single [company account](/account/account-structure#company-account), and one or more sub-accounts called [merchant accounts](/account/account-structure#merchant-accounts). However, in Salesforce, you can only have one merchant account for each storefront.
