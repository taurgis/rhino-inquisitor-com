## Detailed

### 7.2 Limitations of Our Experiments

In the experiment in [Section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.10076#S4 "4 Verbosity Preference of LLMs ‣ Verbosity Bias in Preference Labeling by Large Language Models"), we generate the sample responses from the same questions (before concatenation of results from all three questions). However, in our experiment in [Section 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.10076#S5 "5 Is There A Difference in Verbosity Preference Between LLMs and Humans? ‣ Verbosity Bias in Preference Labeling by Large Language Models"), we mix together results from various questions. This has led us to only attain the result across many kinds of questions, not the result on any specific question like in the experiment in [Section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.10076#S4 "4 Verbosity Preference of LLMs ‣ Verbosity Bias in Preference Labeling by Large Language Models"). It is debatable which of these results is preferable.
