## Appendix C Text Generation Details

Here we describe the data generation procedure for the News and MS MARCO datasets. For News, we use the articles first introduced in [Russell et al. 2025]: these are news articles generated by GPT-4o, Claude-3.5- Sonnet, and O1-pro. The articles are generated by providing the title of a real news article pulled 8 American publications: Associated Press, Discover Magazine, National Geographic, New York Times, Reader’s Digest, Scientific American, Smithsonian Magazine, and Wall Street Journal. We prompt 4 models (OLMo-2-7B-Instruct, Mistral-7B, Gemma-27B, GPT4o-mini) to generate a response using the following prompt:

> You are given a search query and a set of potentially relevant articles. Your task is to answer the query. Sources: \[SOURCES\] Query: \[QUERY\]

Where we replace SOURCES and QUERY with the relevant context and query from the dataset. For all models (where possible), we greedily generate the responses (e.g., setting the parameter for sampling to False). For open-source models, we use the HuggingFace platform to generate the text.1212 12 [https://huggingface.co/]
