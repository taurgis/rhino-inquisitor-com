## Certification Maintenance Overview

In order to maintain your Salesforce certification(s), all certified professionals must successfully complete Trailhead maintenance modules specific to their certification.
  
Keep in mind: If you don’t complete your maintenance requirements by your assigned deadline, your [certification(s) will expire].
  

## Accredited Professional Certification Maintenance

We’re working hard to revamp the entire certification maintenance program, including the Accredited Professional (AP) program.

Certification maintenance is available for the [Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Developer], [Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Catalyst Consultant], and [Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Hyperautomation Developer] with the Winter ‘26 maintenance cycle, [Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Platform Integration Architect] with the Spring ‘26 maintenance cycle, and [Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Platform Architect] with the Summer ‘25 maintenance cycle.

**NOTE:** Please be sure to complete all four modules included in the Tableau Certification Maintenance (Winter '26) badge.

## Certification Maintenance Overview

In order to maintain your Salesforce certification(s), all certified professionals must successfully complete Trailhead maintenance modules specific to their certification. All certifications require completion of **one maintenance badge per year**. Learn more about [your maintenance requirements](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/help?article=Maintaining-Your-Salesforce-Credential).   
  
Keep in mind: If you don’t complete your maintenance requirements by your assigned deadline, your [certification(s) will expire](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/help?article=Information-about-Certification-Expiration).  
  

## Accredited Professional Certification Maintenance

We’re working hard to revamp the entire certification maintenance program, including the Accredited Professional (AP) program. At this time, you don’t need to take any action to maintain your AP certification status. We will share more details about the new maintenance plan when they’re available.  
  

## MuleSoft Certification Maintenance

Certification maintenance is available for the [Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Developer](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/mulesoft-developer-certification-maintenance-winter-26), [Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Catalyst Consultant](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/mulesoft-catalyst-consultant-certification-maintenance-winter-26), and [Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Hyperautomation Developer](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/mulesoft-hyperautomation-developer-certification-maintenance-winter-26) with the Winter ‘26 maintenance cycle, [Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Platform Integration Architect](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/mulesoft-platform-integration-architect-certification-maintenance-spring-26) with the Spring ‘26 maintenance cycle, and [Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Platform Architect](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/mulesoft-platform-architect-certification-maintenance-summer-25) with the Summer ‘25 maintenance cycle.  
  

## Tableau Certification Maintenance

Certification maintenance is offered through the [Tableau Certification Maintenance (Winter '26) badge](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/tableau-certification-maintenance-winter-26) for the Salesforce Certified Tableau Architect, Salesforce Certified Tableau Consultant, Salesforce Certified Tableau Data Analyst, and Salesforce Certified Tableau Server Administrator.   
  
**NOTE:** Please be sure to complete all four modules included in the Tableau Certification Maintenance (Winter '26) badge.
