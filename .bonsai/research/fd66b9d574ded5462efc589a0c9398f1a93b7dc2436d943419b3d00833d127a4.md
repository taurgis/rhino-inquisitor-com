### A.4 Consistency Between the Theoretical Bias Estimator and Implementation

In our experimental implementation, for a judge model AA, we obtain the probabilities assigned to tokens A and B for each individual instruction. The token with the higher probability is selected as the winner. By aggregating the outcomes over all instructions, we compute the win rate, which can be formulated as 𝔼x\[𝕀\[σ(δ+bA)\>0.5\]\]\\mathbb{E}\_{x}\[\\mathbb{I}\[\\sigma(\\delta+b\_{A})>0.5\]\], where 𝕀\\mathbb{I} is the indicator function. Specifically, it can be seen as an approximation to sampling from a Bernoulli distribution with success probability σ⁡(δ+bA)\\sigma(\\delta+b\_{A}). The same applies to gold judge models.
