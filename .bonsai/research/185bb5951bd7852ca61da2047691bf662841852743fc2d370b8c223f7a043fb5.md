# Composable Storefront Release Notes

These release notes include updates to Progressive Web App (PWA) Kit and Managed Runtime.

:::tip The latest PWA Kit release notes are published on the [releases page on GitHub] before they’re published here. To stay informed about new PWA Kit releases, subscribe to GitHub notifications for releases.

:::note
- Release notes may be published before the relevant Github packages are updated. The release notes identify any applicable package version that a feature is available in.
- For the general B2C Commerce release notes, see [Salesforce Help].

## 7/7/2026

### Maintain Shopper Sessions Across Subdomains

Configure a shared parent cookie domain to keep shoppers logged in and preserve their baskets when navigating between different subdomains in hybrid PWA Kit and SFRA storefronts.

**Where:** This change applies to Composable Storefronts in B2C Commerce.

**How**: In Business Manager, go to **Merchant Tools > *Your Site***.

**See Also**

- [GitHub: Hybrid Cookie Domain Configuration]

### Use HttpOnly Session Cookies for Improved Storefront Security

When you enable HttpOnly session cookies, the storefront stores Shopper Login and API Access Service (SLAS) access and refresh tokens as HttpOnly cookies instead of local storage. JavaScript code that runs on the page can't access them.

**Where:** This change applies to Composable Storefronts in B2C Commerce.

**How**: Turn on HttpOnly cookies with the `enableHttpOnlySessionCookies` app configuration in `config/default.js`.

**See Also**

- [GitHub: HttpOnly Session Cookies — Architecture]
- [GitHub: HttpOnly Mode: SLAS Property Storage Mapping]

Managing orders requires that you connect your B2C Commerce instance to the Salesforce Order Management organization.

**Where:** This change applies to Composable Storefronts in B2C Commerce.

## 7/6/2026

### Control Proxy User-Agent Forwarding

Control whether proxied requests forward the shopper's original browser user agent or replace it with an infrastructure user agent.

- **Browser**: Forwards the shopper's original user agent, including device and browser details. Use this setting if your backend personalizes responses based on device type or browser.
- **Infrastructure**: Replaces the user agent with the Amazon CloudFront value, hiding shopper device details from your backend.

Set the default for all new environments in **Project Settings**, or override it per environment in **Environment Settings > Advanced**.

**Where:** This change applies to Managed Runtime.

**See Also**

- [Managed Runtime Administration]
- [Proxy Requests]

## 11/4/2025

### Provide Rule-Based Promotions for Bonus Products in Your Storefront

Rule-based bonus product promotions expand the promotions you can offer to customers natively in your storefront without writing any custom code. Previously, only list-based bonus product promotions were available, which let shoppers choose bonus products from a list of prespecified items.

Keep these considerations in mind when using rule-based bonus product promotions.

- Only one bonus product promotion is supported for a qualifying item. If a product qualifies for a rule-based and a list-based bonus product promotion, only one promotion takes effect and it's the promotion with the highest priority. For example, one promotion grants the shopper two free ties of their choice when buying $200 worth of men's clothing. A higher priority promotion grants the shopper a free pair of shoes when buying a men's suit. When the shopper adds a men's suit to their cart worth $200, they can't get both promotions—they get only the free pair of shoes and not the two free ties.
- Cart-level choice of bonus product promotions aren't supported. Automatic bonus product promotions are supported.

**See Also**

- *Salesforce Help*: [Choice of Bonus Product Discount in B2C Commerce]

### Test Your Hybrid App in Secondary Instance Group (SIG) Instances with Hybrid Proxy

SIG instances don't have an eCDN to route traffic to SFRA and Managed Runtime (MRT) at the same time but they can use hybrid proxy for traffic routing.

## 7/7/2026

### Maintain Shopper Sessions Across Subdomains

Configure a shared parent cookie domain to keep shoppers logged in and preserve their baskets when navigating between different subdomains in hybrid PWA Kit and SFRA storefronts. Without this configuration, shoppers lose their login status and shopping baskets when crossing subdomain boundaries, such as `www.example.com` and `shop.example.com`. The loss of login status happens because browsers isolate cookies to individual hosts by default. Prevent frustration and abandoned carts by setting up a shared parent cookie domain.

**Where:** This change applies to Composable Storefronts in B2C Commerce.

**How**: In Business Manager, go to **Merchant Tools > *Your Site***. In the quick-find box, search for `hybrid auth` and then select **Hybrid Auth Settings**.

**See Also**

- [GitHub: Hybrid Cookie Domain Configuration](https://github.com/SalesforceCommerceCloud/pwa-kit/blob/develop/docs/hybrid-cookie-domain-configuration.md)

### Use HttpOnly Session Cookies for Improved Storefront Security

When you enable HttpOnly session cookies, the storefront stores Shopper Login and API Access Service (SLAS) access and refresh tokens as HttpOnly cookies instead of local storage. The server-side SLAS proxy layer handles token injection transparently. Only browsers can read and send HttpOnly cookies. JavaScript code that runs on the page can't access them. Using HttpOnly cookies prevents client-side scripts from exploiting the storefront and makes the storefront more secure.

**Where:** This change applies to Composable Storefronts in B2C Commerce.

**How**: Turn on HttpOnly cookies with the `enableHttpOnlySessionCookies` app configuration in `config/default.js`.

**See Also**

- [GitHub: HttpOnly Session Cookies — Architecture](https://github.com/SalesforceCommerceCloud/pwa-kit/blob/develop/docs/httponly-cookies-architecture.md)
- [GitHub: HttpOnly Mode: SLAS Property Storage Mapping](https://github.com/SalesforceCommerceCloud/pwa-kit/blob/develop/docs/httponly-storage-mapping.md)

### Control Proxy User-Agent Forwarding

Control whether proxied requests forward the shopper's original browser user agent or replace it with an infrastructure user agent. The new **Proxy User Agent** setting gives your backend control over how much device and browser information it receives from proxied requests.

- **Browser**: Forwards the shopper's original user agent, including device and browser details. Use this setting if your backend personalizes responses based on device type or browser.
- **Infrastructure**: Replaces the user agent with the Amazon CloudFront value, hiding shopper device details from your backend.

Set the default for all new environments in **Project Settings**, or override it per environment in **Environment Settings > Advanced**.

**Where:** This change applies to Managed Runtime.

### Provide Rule-Based Promotions for Bonus Products in Your Storefront

Rule-based bonus product promotions expand the promotions you can offer to customers natively in your storefront without writing any custom code. Previously, only list-based bonus product promotions were available, which let shoppers choose bonus products from a list of prespecified items. With rule-based bonus product promotions, when a shopper's order meets the promotion's conditions, the shopper can choose any bonus product from a certain category or segment that meets the discounted product rule. For example, if a shopper adds $200 worth of men's clothing to their cart, they can pick two free ties of their choice.

Keep these considerations in mind when using rule-based bonus product promotions.

- Only one bonus product promotion is supported for a qualifying item. If a product qualifies for a rule-based and a list-based bonus product promotion, only one promotion takes effect and it's the promotion with the highest priority. For example, one promotion grants the shopper two free ties of their choice when buying $200 worth of men's clothing. A higher priority promotion grants the shopper a free pair of shoes when buying a men's suit. When the shopper adds a men's suit to their cart worth $200, they can't get both promotions—they get only the free pair of shoes and not the two free ties.
- Cart-level choice of bonus product promotions aren't supported. Automatic bonus product promotions are supported.

### PWA Kit - Control the Hydration Behavior of Your Composable Storefront and Boost your Site's Performance

With the `<Island/>` component, you get fine-grained control over when specific server-rendered components are hydrated on the client. This targeted hydration approach improves performance by avoiding unnecessary computation and reducing time to interactivity. By deferring hydration to only essential components, you benefit from server-rendering at scale without the overhead of hydrating the entire page at once. The `<Island/>` component is inspired by Astro's Islands architecture. ### Managed Runtime - CDN Performance Enhancements

Get reduced page load times after redeploying your bundles. These Content Delivery Network (CDN) enhancements improve app performance:
