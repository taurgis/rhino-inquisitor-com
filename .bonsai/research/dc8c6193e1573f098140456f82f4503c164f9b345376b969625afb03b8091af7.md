## Summary

Third-Party Applications > Google Channels Integration > Update Cartridge Paths

## Compressed

### Update Cartridge Paths

1. In **Business Manager**, click App Launcher  and then select **Administration > Sites > Manage Sites**.

2. Select your site.

3. Click **Settings**.

4. In the Cartridges field, add the new cartridges. For example, `path:int_socialfeeds:bm_socialfeeds:bc_library:bm_socialchannelsint_socialfeeds`

   - `bm_socialfeeds`
   - `bc_library`
   - `bm_socialchannels`

5. Update your Site cartridge path:

   1. In Business Manager, click App Launcher  and then select **Administration > Sites > Manage Sites**.
   2. Select your site.
   3. Click **Settings**.
   4. In the Cartridges field, add the following cartridges.

   For example, `path: int_google:int_socialfeeds:bc_library:app_storefront_baseint_google`

   - `int_google`
   - `int_socialfeeds`
   - `bc_library`
