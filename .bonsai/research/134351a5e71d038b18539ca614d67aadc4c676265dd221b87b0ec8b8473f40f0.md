### Generative Model

Motivated by the successful description of the stretched exponential distribution (2), we search for a generative stochastic process that can model word usage. We consider the inverse frequency ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e075) as given and focus on describing how the words are distributed throughout the text. We assume that our text (abstractly regarded as arbitrarily long) is generated by a well-defined stationary stochastic process with finite ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e076) for the words of interest. We further assume that the probability ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e077) of using the word ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e078) depends only on the distance ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e079) since the last occurrence of the word. The latter means that we are modeling the word usage as a _renewal process_ [\[34\]](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0007678#pone.0007678-Cox1), [\[36\]](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0007678#pone.0007678-Santhanam1). The distribution of recurrence times is then given by the (joint) probability of having the word at distance ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e080) and not having this word for ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e081):![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e082)The cumulative distribution function is written as![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e083)(3)The time dependent probability ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e084), also known as _hazard function_, can be obtained empirically as ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e085) (see [Text S1](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0007678#pone.0007678.s001), _Hazard Function_). Equation (3) reduces to the exponential distribution (1) for a time independent probability ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e086). The Weibull renewal process we propose can be analyzed formally as a particular instance of a doubly stochastic Poisson process [\[41\]](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0007678#pone.0007678-Yannaros1).
