## Summary

Composable Storefront Release Notes > 7/7/2026 > Managing Orders in Your Composable Storefront Is Available for Shoppers

## Compressed

### Managing Orders in Your Composable Storefront Is Available for Shoppers

The Salesforce Order Management (SOM) integration in your Composable Storefront lets registered shoppers manage orders stored in SOM. PWA Kit provides default implementations for the order actions in SOM so you don't have to implement them. The actions available for shoppers are tracking order shipments, canceling an order, and returning order items. Managing orders requires that you connect your B2C Commerce instance to the Salesforce Order Management organization.

**Where:** This change applies to Composable Storefronts in B2C Commerce.
