## Results and Discussion

[Figure 1](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0007678#pone-0007678-g001) shows the empirical results obtained for the example words _theory_ and _also_ in the talk.origins group of the USENET database. Both words have ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e032) but are linguistically quite different: while _theory_ is a common noun, _also_ is an adverb that functions semantically as an operator. The deviation from the Poisson prediction (1) is apparent in [Fig. 1(a–c)](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0007678#pone-0007678-g001): ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e033) is larger than the exponential distribution for distances ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e034) both much shorter and much longer than ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e035), while it is smaller for ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e036). Both words exhibit a most probable recurrence time ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e037) and a monotonically decaying distribution ![](article/file?type=thumbnail&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007678.e038) for larger times \[[Fig. 1(a)](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0007678#pone-0007678-g001)\]. Comparing the insets in [Fig. 1(b)](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0007678#pone-0007678-g001), one sees that the occurrences of _theory_ are clustered close to each other in a phenomenon known as burstiness [\[6\]](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0007678#pone.0007678-Goh1), [\[14\]](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0007678#pone.0007678-Church1), [\[15\]](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0007678#pone.0007678-Katz1), [\[19\]](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0007678#pone.0007678-Sarkar1), [\[21\]](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0007678#pone.0007678-Serrano1). Due to burstiness, the frequency of the word _theory_ estimated from a small sample would differ a great deal as a function of exactly where the sample was drawn. Similar but lesser deviations are observed for the word _also_.
