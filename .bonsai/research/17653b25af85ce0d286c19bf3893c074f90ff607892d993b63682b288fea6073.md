# 26.9 Release

This B2C Commerce release occurs between August 18 and October 1, 2026.

*   **[Manage Commerce Apps from a Dedicated Business Manager Workspace]**  
    Discover, install, and manage Commerce Apps across every feature domain from one location. Browse the full app catalog, discover apps in a featured apps carousel, and get quick access to documentation before you install. The App Catalog brings your native, partner, and custom apps into one list and tracks each app's setup progress.
*   **[Honor Shopper Consents Across Notification Channels]**  
    You can create a flow so that Data Cloud syncs shopper consent preferences to B2C Commerce automatically. When a shopper changes email or text message notification preferences, a consent sync flow updates the shopper's record in B2C Commerce. Use this feature to protect shopper trust and stay compliant with consent preference regulations.
*   **[Build Your Consented Marketing Audience at SFRA Checkout]**  
    SFRA storefronts now support shopper consent capture at checkout. Capture consents at the moment shoppers place their orders without interrupting order placement.
*   **[Access Tools from One Place with the Business Manager Utility Bar]**  
    Reach your most-used tools from anywhere in Business Manager with the Utility Bar. Review alerts, access tools, preview a storefront, and manage your user preferences, all from a single bar. Previously, these tools were on the Business Manager header.
*   **[Manage Multiple Tax Items on a Basket Line Item]**  
    Track up to 10 individual tax items on a basket line item, in addition to the standard aggregated tax value. Each tax item includes an ID, value, and rate, and depending on your requirements, can capture the taxes that apply to where you do business, such as federal, state, or district. Tax items automatically sum into the existing aggregated tax fields without adding fields at the order-totals level, so you manage either the aggregated tax field or the individual tax items, not both.
*   **[Avoid Data Residency and Privacy Issues with Salesforce Connection]**  
    Before you connect your B2C Commerce instance to a Salesforce org, an advisory message appears if the regions don’t match, or if B2C Commerce can’t determine the region. Review the advisory message and verify that your cross-region configuration meets data residency and privacy requirements.
