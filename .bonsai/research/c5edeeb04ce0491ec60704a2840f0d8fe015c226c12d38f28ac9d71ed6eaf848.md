## Edit summaries

*   [WP:AISUMMARY](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:AISUMMARY&redirect=no)
*   [WP:AISUMM](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:AISUMM&redirect=no)

AI [edit summaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Edit_summary "Help:Edit summary") tend to follow "templates" with distinct idiosyncracies that are uncommon to nonexistent in edit summaries prior to 2023. The presence of such summaries strongly suggests that the edits themselves are also AI-generated, as it is unlikely someone would use AI for a simple summary but not the much more time-consuming task of writing.

Note that these are _rigid formulae_. The more a summary deviates from the pattern, the less likely it is to be AI-generated -- even if the text is long or formal in tone. Edit summaries with [common abbreviations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Edit_summary_legend "Wikipedia:Edit summary legend"), such as "ce" for "copy edit," are also unlikely to be wholly AI-generated.

As with other LLM-generated text, typical AI edit summaries have changed over time. Older AI edit summaries are often written in first-person with the same signs as AI article text, such as [AI vocabulary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:AIVOCAB "Wikipedia:AIVOCAB"), [emoji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:AIEMOJI "Wikipedia:AIEMOJI"), (attempted) [list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:AILIST "Wikipedia:AILIST") formatting, or [markdown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:MARKDOWN "Wikipedia:MARKDOWN") formatting.[\[i\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing#cite_note-41) Newer AI summaries are less likely to contain those signs, but more likely to be exhaustively procedural about the editing process and how it complies with Wikipedia guidelines and policies.
