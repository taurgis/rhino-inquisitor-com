## Update: 26 January 2026

MDN uses `display: contents` on the `<main>` of its content pages (`//main[@id="content"]`). This _might_ be messing with the heuristics that Safari on iOS 15 uses when showing a page in its reader view. This is [based on some testing from Jonathan Schofield]. It could also be related to something else. I’m not a WebKit rendering engine engineer. Either way, with people [avoiding upgrading to the latest iOS] or simply unable to buy a new phone, it could prove to be a lingering problem.
