The lack of significant quality differences between LLM writing is somewhat unexpected and warrants deeper examination. Several factors however may explain this surprising finding. Books play a crucial role in the training of generative AI systems. Their long, thematically consistent paragraphs provide information about how to generate coherent and fluent text. All three models (GPT-4, Claude 3.5, Llama 3.1) are pre-trained on Books3corpus ([Kelly, 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.14509v5#bib.bib55)) which constitute a bigger portion of Pile ([Gao et al., 2020](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.14509v5#bib.bib42)) pre-training data.
