## Compressed

### 4.1 Statistics

The non-adversarial portion of the RAID dataset consists of 509,014 generations and 14,971 human-written documents for a total of 6,287,820 texts when including adversarial attacks. On average, models are more repetitive than humans as measured by Self-BLEU [Zhu et al. (2018)] and typically generate shorter passages (see Table [3]). The mean perplexity is lower for models than humans, according to LLaMA 7B and GPT 2 XL.
