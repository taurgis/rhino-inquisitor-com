## Compressed

B2C Commerce

B2C Commerce is a cloud-based ecommerce platform that allows businesses to create and manage their online stores. The developer experience for B2C Commerce is designed to provide a flexible, scalable, and customizable platform that can meet the needs of businesses of all sizes.

Get StartedAPI Reference

Key Features

*   Customize your storefront
    
    With B2C Commerce, developers can customize the look and feel of their online stores, create custom functionality, and integrate third-party services. The platform provides a robust set of APIs and webhooks to enable integration with external systems.
    
*   Performance, scalability, and security
    
    B2C Commerce is designed to provide high performance and scalability for online stores. The platform uses a distributed architecture that can handle high traffic volumes and provides automatic scaling of resources. And with features like two-factor authentication, role-based access control, and data encryption, your storefront is safe and secure.
    
*   Community
    
    B2C Commerce has a large community of developers and partners who contribute to the platform's ecosystem. The community provides resources, tools, and best practices for developers to help them build and maintain their online stores.
