# Deliver Smarter Search Results with Agentic Commerce Search

## Detailed

# Deliver Smarter Search Results with Agentic Commerce Search

Agentic Commerce Search replaces native keyword search with an AI model trained on your catalog data, shopper behavior, and simulated queries. It ranks products by relevance and conversion, handling natural language, typos, and emerging trends without manual maintenance. With Agentic Commerce Search, you no longer need to maintain synonym lists, stop word lists, and search rules for every edge case.

**Where:** This change applies to B2C Commerce. Agentic Commerce Search is available for an extra cost. To purchase the add-on, contact your Salesforce account executive.

**When:** Agentic Commerce Search is available in July 2026.

**How:** In Business Manager, from **Merchant Tools** | **Agentic Commerce Search**, go to **Search Preferences**, and turn on **Enable Agentic Commerce Search**.
