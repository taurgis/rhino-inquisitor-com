## Compressed

### Horizontal Alignment

I generally left-align all my text (not justified, because justified text adversely affects dyslexic readers) and right align my numbers (because browsers [refuse to support decimal alignment]). Then I find my exceptions and adjust accordingly for both the column header and the cell.

th,
td {
  text-align: left;
}
th:nth-of-type(3),
td:nth-of-type(3) {
  text-align: right;
}

Seriously, [Designing Tables to be Read, Not Looked At] came out after I wrote all this stuff. Really. Anyway, he goes into more detail than is relevant here.
