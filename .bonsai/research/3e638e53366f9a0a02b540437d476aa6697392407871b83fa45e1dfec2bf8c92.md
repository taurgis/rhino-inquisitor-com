## 6 Conclusion

In this paper, we introduce LongJudgeBench, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating LLM-as-a-judge reliability in long-form output evaluation. Covering diverse real-world scenarios and evaluation protocols, LongJudgeBench enables systematic analysis of LLM judges across different settings. Our experiments show that long-form judging remains highly challenging, and we hope this work will help pave the way for future research on reliable LLM-as-a-judge methods.
