## Summary

Required conditions for origin trials > The origin trial is ongoing

## Compressed

### The origin trial is ongoing

You can check the end date for an origin trial from its [registration page]:

![Chrome Origin Trials
page for First Party Sets & SameParty with Trial Available details highlighted.](/static/docs/web-platform/origin-trial-troubleshooting/image/chrome-origin-trials-page-e19512a6ec5fc.png)

For trials that have ended, DevTools displays something like this:

![Chrome DevTools 
origin trials information in the Application panel showing ValidTokenNotProvided and Status Expired](/static/docs/web-platform/origin-trial-troubleshooting/image/chrome-devtools-origin-t-12b4868247e75.png)

You are sent automated emails when feedback is required or a token is about to expire, but not when the trial ends.
