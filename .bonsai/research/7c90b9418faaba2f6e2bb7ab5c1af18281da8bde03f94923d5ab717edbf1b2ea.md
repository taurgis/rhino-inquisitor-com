## Compressed

# Access Tools from One Place with the Business Manager Utility Bar

Reach your most-used tools from anywhere in Business Manager with the Utility Bar. Review alerts, access tools, preview a storefront, and manage your user preferences, all from a single bar. Previously, these tools were on the Business Manager header.

**Where:** This change applies to Business Manager in B2C Commerce.

**How:** To open a tool, click its icon on the **Utility Bar** along the left side of Business Manager.
