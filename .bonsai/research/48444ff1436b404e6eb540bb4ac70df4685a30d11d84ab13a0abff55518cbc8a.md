## Compressed

### 2.3 Direction Agreement

Direction agreement (Table 2 in main text) is computed as within-model comparison: for each model, each marker’s direction under rewrite-only or voice-preserving is compared to that same model’s direction under generic, then averaged across models. This measures whether prompt condition affects the direction of change for individual models, not cross-model consensus.
