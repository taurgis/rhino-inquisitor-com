## Limitations

Our study is limited to articles in American English, chosen for their consistent formatting and high quality (i.e., professionally written and proofread). We also did not investigate factual accuracy, as it did not appear to be a significant cue for our annotators, who covered a broad range of topics. While we selected articles from reputable sources, there remains a possibility that some included AI-generated edits beyond our scope of detection.
