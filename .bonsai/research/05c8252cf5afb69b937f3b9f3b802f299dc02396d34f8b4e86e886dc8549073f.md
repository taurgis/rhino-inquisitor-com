# 26.8 Release

This B2C Commerce release occurs between July 21 and August 20, 2026.

*   **[Maintain Consistent Product Information Across Online and Store Channels]**  
    Keep your product data synced between B2C Commerce and Point of Sale with catalog unification. B2C Commerce becomes your single source of truth and automatically syncs product information to Point of Sale on your schedule. Eliminate manual data entry, reduce errors, and make sure that customers see accurate product details whether they shop online or in-store.
*   **[Catch System Security Issues Early with Anomaly Detection]**  
    Identify performance regressions and security anomalies, such as error spikes or failed logins, earlier by running an Anomaly Detection log. You no longer need to manually investigate issues. B2C Commerce analyzes log patterns and shows anomalies in real time.
*   **[Evaluate Click-Through Rate in Search Results]**  
    Monitor how often shoppers click a result after searching. Use the new Click-Through Rate metric on the On-Site–Top Search dashboard to review search result engagement and spot trends and prioritize conversion opportunities.
*   **[Access Agentic Commerce Search Directly from the App Launcher]**  
    Open Agentic Commerce Search directly from the App Launcher without navigating through Merchant Tools.
*   **[Design Your Own Merchandising Workspace]**  
    Create a unified view and customized navigation tailored to your merchandising tasks by using the Merchandising template or build a custom workspace from scratch using Manual Setup.
*   **[Rename and Delete Apps Directly from the App Header]**  
    Manage your custom Business Manager apps more efficiently by renaming or removing them directly from the app header. Rename an app to match your business terminology, or permanently remove an app you no longer use to reduce workspace clutter.
*   **[Work More Efficiently with the Redesigned Business Manager Interface]**  
    Access version, updates, and POD details in the Business Manager navigation panel instead of the footer, which has been removed to reduce clutter. Instance and site time zone are available in a tooltip.
*   **[Boost Productivity with Unified Header Search and Redesigned Navigation]**  
    Streamline navigation and find tools faster with a new search bar in the header and an updated left navigation menu in Business Manager. Use header search to find pages across Merchant Tools and Administration from one combined list, without opening the waffle menu first. The updated left navigation provides more vertical space for nested categories and improves scanability with tighter spacing, left-aligned accordion toggles, and a higher-contrast layout.
*   **[Add and Move Modules to Match Your Workflow]**  
    Organize pages and modules to fit your workflow by dragging them into your preferred order.
*   **[Group Pages and Modules into Sections to Improve Navigation]**  
    Tailor your workspace by grouping pages and modules into custom sections. Create, rename, and remove sections to build a navigation structure that matches your workflow.
*   **[Monitor Commerce App Integration Health with Connection Status Reporting]**  
    Assess whether your Commerce apps are healthy, degraded, unhealthy, or unknown directly on the installed app screen in Business Manager. Catch broken integrations early with real-time visibility, clear error messages, and timestamps that pinpoint when issues occurred. Resolve connection problems faster with specific remediation steps, such as reauthenticating or updating credentials, before they affect your storefront operations.
