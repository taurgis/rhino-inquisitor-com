### AI-Generated Text Is Everywhere

In a separate [study](https://graphite.io/five-percent/ai-now-writes-as-many-online-articles-as-humans-do), we show that half of the articles being published online are AI-generated. Sites like [Reddit](https://www.wired.com/story/ai-slop-is-ruining-reddit-for-everyone/) and [arXiv](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/research-repository-arxiv-will-ban-authors-for-a-year-if-they-let-ai-do-all-the-work/) are grappling with how to reduce the amount of AI-generated content they receive.

AI is also increasingly used in the workplace. About half of US employees now use it at least occasionally, up from a fifth two years ago, and [one in eight use it daily](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/701195/frequent-workplace-continued-rise.aspx).
