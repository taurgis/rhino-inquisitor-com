## Summary

Set up the cartridge > Step 7: Set up in-person payments > Enable in-person payments

## Compressed

### Enable in-person payments

1. In the Salesforce Business Manager, go to **Merchant Tools** > **Ordering** > **Payment methods**.
2. Select **AdyenPOS**.
3. Select **Yes** in the **Enabled** column.
4. From the **Payment Processor** drop-down in the **AdyenPOS Details** section, select **Adyen\_POS**.
