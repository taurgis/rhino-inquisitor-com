## Compressed

## `on.workflow_call`

Use `on.workflow_call` to define the inputs and outputs for a reusable workflow. You can also map the secrets that are available to the called workflow. For more information on reusable workflows, see [Reuse workflows].

## Detailed

## `on.workflow_call`

Use `on.workflow_call` to define the inputs and outputs for a reusable workflow. You can also map the secrets that are available to the called workflow. For more information on reusable workflows, see [Reuse workflows](/en/actions/how-tos/reuse-automations/reuse-workflows).
