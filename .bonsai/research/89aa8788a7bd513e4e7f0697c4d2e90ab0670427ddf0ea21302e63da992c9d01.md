## Summary

The Advantages of SCAPI > Migrate to SCAPI: The Modern Standard for B2C Commerce > Transition Guidance

## Compressed

### Transition Guidance

- **New Projects**: All new implementations must use SCAPI exclusively.
- **Existing Projects**: If you are currently using OCAPI, you must begin planning your migration to SCAPI.
- **Note**: Per the [OCAPI versioning and deprecation policy], Salesforce isn't establishing an end-of-life (EOL) date to retire OCAPI. This decision is subject to annual review. Salesforce sets any designated EOL date no earlier than two years from the date it formally decides to retire OCAPI. Until then, OCAPI receives bug fixes and security updates where applicable, and no further innovations.
- **Hybrid Transition**: During your migration, use the Shopper Login and API Access Service (SLAS) to bridge both API sets. SLAS allows you to use a single authentication token across both OCAPI and SCAPI, enabling a phased, low-risk transition.
