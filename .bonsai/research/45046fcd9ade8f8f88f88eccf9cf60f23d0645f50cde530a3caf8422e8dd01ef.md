### 6.4. Qualitative Insights into LLM edits

Given how LLM-edited paragraphs are often preferred over default LLM-generated ones and sometimes even Writer edited paragraphs, we inspect if there are specific categories of edits where LLMs perform better, more so if that caters to a certain genre. To edit Purple Prose models mostly need to learn how to simplify the text (i.e., generate a paraphrase in simple language). LLMs are effective at Sentence Simplification ([Dou et al., 2024]) so this doesn’t come across as a surprising finding.
