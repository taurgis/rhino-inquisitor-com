Composable Storefront Release Notes > 3/12/2026 > Integrate Salesforce Payments into Composable Storefronts for a Seamless Checkout Experience

## Compressed

### Integrate Salesforce Payments into Composable Storefronts for a Seamless Checkout Experience

Streamline the checkout experience for shoppers with a native Salesforce Payments solution that supports multiple payment methods and payment gateways such as Adyen, Stripe, and PayPal.

**Where:** This change applies to Composable Storefronts in B2C Commerce.

**How:** This feature is available for Composable Storefronts after the B2C Commerce 26.3 release and the PWA Kit version 3.17 release. To enable Salesforce Payments for Composable Storefronts, see [Set Up Salesforce Payments for B2C Commerce].

**See Also**

- [Configure Salesforce Payments for Your B2C Commerce Composable Storefronts]

## Detailed

### Integrate Salesforce Payments into Composable Storefronts for a Seamless Checkout Experience

Streamline the checkout experience for shoppers with a native Salesforce Payments solution that supports multiple payment methods and payment gateways such as Adyen, Stripe, and PayPal. Reduce the complexity and cost of implementing payments and provide developers with extensive customization capabilities.

**Where:** This change applies to Composable Storefronts in B2C Commerce.

**How:** This feature is available for Composable Storefronts after the B2C Commerce 26.3 release and the PWA Kit version 3.17 release. To enable Salesforce Payments for Composable Storefronts, see [Set Up Salesforce Payments for B2C Commerce](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=cc.b2c_setting_up_salesforce_payments.htm).
