## Summary

Required conditions for origin trials > Subdomain matching is enabled for a token used on a subdomain

## Compressed

### Subdomain matching is enabled for a token used on a subdomain

If an origin trial feature doesn't seem to be working for some pages on your site, check that tokens are correctly set up for the subdomains serving them.

When you register for an origin trial, you can optionally choose to match all subdomains of the origin:

![Chrome origin trials registration page showing subdomain-matching selected](/static/docs/web-platform/origin-trial-troubleshooting/image/chrome-origin-trials-reg-1b02f81dbb776.png)

You can also match subdomains for third-party tokens:

![Chrome origin trials registration page showing third-party matching and subdomain-matching selected](/static/docs/web-platform/origin-trial-troubleshooting/image/chrome-origin-trials-reg-51e8730baaa9e.png)

Subdomain tokens [are not issued] for origins in the [Public Suffix List]. For example, you cannot register an origin such as `https://appspot.com` or `https://github.io`, though you can register for domains within that origin, such as `https://example.appspot.com` or `https://example.github.io`.
