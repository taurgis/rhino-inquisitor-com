### Overemphasis on presence/reliability of citations

One of the common general signs of AI generated text is the tendency to [allude to the existence of coverage but not actually summarise the content of that coverage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:AIATTR "Wikipedia:AIATTR"). A similar form of this manifests in edit summaries, where an excessive focus is placed on the fact that the content added is "sourced", "closely attributed" to "reliable sources", that "citations" or "coverage" were added, and so on. They will often note that they are independent or secondary sources, echoing the guidelines on notability or reliability.

A human editor is far more likely to focus on the actual content of the prose they added (e.g., "Added info about the artist's debut") rather than only vaguely mentioning the source that information is linked to.
