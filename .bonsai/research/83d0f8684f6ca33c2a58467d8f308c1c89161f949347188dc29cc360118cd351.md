Composable Storefront Release Notes > 7/6/2026 > Fix Redirect Operations for Paths with URL-Reserved Characters

## Compressed

### Fix Redirect Operations for Paths with URL-Reserved Characters

Deleting, editing, or reading redirect rules that include URL-reserved characters (such as `%`, `#`, or `?`) now works correctly in Runtime Admin. Previously, these operations could fail silently for redirects whose paths included encoded or reserved characters.

**Where:** This change applies to Managed Runtime.
