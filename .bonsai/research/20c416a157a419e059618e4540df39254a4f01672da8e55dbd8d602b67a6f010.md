## Build healthy solutions with the Well-Architected Framework.

The Well-Architected Framework helps architects build trusted, easy, and adaptable solutions.

## Trusted

Trusted solutions protect the business, users, and data ensuring solutions are secure, compliant, and reliable by design.

Ensure only the right users and systems have access, while protecting data from exposure, misuse, and threats.

Design solutions that meet legal, regulatory, ethical, and accessibility standards across industries and regions.

Build architectures that remain available, performant, and resilient as demand, scale, and conditions change.

## Easy

Easy solutions deliver value fast, ensuring solutions are intentional, automated, and engaging by design.

Design solutions that prioritize business value, balance trade offs, and remain understandable and maintainable over time.

Enable work to happen faster and at scale by reducing manual effort and enforcing consistency through healthy automation.

Create intuitive, user-centered experiences that help users succeed and drive adoption.

## Adaptable

Adaptable solutions evolve with the business, ensuring solutions are resilient and composable by design.

Design architectures that absorb change and recovery quickly from disruption.

Build modular solutions that can be adjusted, extended, or replaced independently without destabilizing the system.

The Salesforce Architecture Center is a centralized hub for trusted architecture guidance, frameworks, and best practices. It helps architects design scalable, secure, and high-performing solutions across the Agentforce 360 Platform using proven [patterns](https://architect.salesforce.com/docs/architect/well-architected-tools/guide/patterns), Decision Guides, the Well-Architected Framework, and more resources.

The [Salesforce Architecture Program](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/relaunching-salesforce-architecture-program/) helps architects navigate today’s architectural challenges and build trusted solutions for tomorrow. Designed to support architects at every stage, the program provides practical guidance, new resources, and opportunities to engage with the community, helping you design for scale, build trusted solutions, and navigate the complexity of the Agentic Enterprise.

The Architecture Center provides [Decision](https://architect.salesforce.com/decision-guides) [Guides](https://architect.salesforce.com/decision-guides), [Fundamentals](https://architect.salesforce.com/fundamentals), and [patterns](https://architect.salesforce.com/docs/architect/well-architected-tools/guide/patterns) that help architects evaluate trade-offs. In addition, architects can learn through [blogs](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/category/architects/), livestream series like [Think Like an Architect](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/think-like-an-architect/), and short-form videos.

Together, these resources cover key domains like data architecture, integration, security, scalability, and AI-driven design, helping architects apply best practices and make informed decisions across the platform.

Choosing the right architecture starts with understanding your business requirements, data flows, and scalability needs. The Architecture Center provides [Decision Guides](https://architect.salesforce.com/decision-guides) and patterns that help architects evaluate trade-offs, select the right approach, and design solutions.

The Architecture Center is designed for Salesforce Architects, developers, administrators, and technical leaders responsible for designing and scaling solutions. Whether you're new to architecture or an experienced enterprise architect, the resources support better decision-making, consistency, and governance.

In the Agentic AI era, Salesforce Architects play a critical role in designing AI-powered, data-driven, and agent-based systems. This includes architecting for real-time data access, orchestration across systems, governance of AI agents, and scalable automation, ensuring solutions remain trusted, adaptable, and future-ready.
