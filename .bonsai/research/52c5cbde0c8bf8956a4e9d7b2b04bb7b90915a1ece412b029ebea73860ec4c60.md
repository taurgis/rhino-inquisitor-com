## 2 Related Work

In Table [1](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07940v1#S1.T1 "Table 1 ‣ 1 Introduction ‣ RAID: A Shared Benchmark for Robust Evaluation of Machine-Generated Text Detectors") we show a comparison between RAID and other publicly available sources of generated text. Among these, the most similar work to ours is [Li et al. (2023b)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07940v1#bib.bib45), who create a dataset of 447k generations from 27 language models across 10 domains to study the robustness of detectors. Other resources typically focus on particular sub-areas such as multilingual text [Macko et al. (2023)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07940v1#bib.bib53); [Wang et al. (2023b)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07940v1#bib.bib86), code [Wang et al. (2023a)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07940v1#bib.bib85), question-answering [Guo et al. (2023)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07940v1#bib.bib28); [Xu et al. (2023)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07940v1#bib.bib91); [Su et al. (2024)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07940v1#bib.bib79), and scientific papers [Mosca et al. (2023)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07940v1#bib.bib58). Additionally, shared tasks such as AuTextTification [Sarvazyan et al. (2023a)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07940v1#bib.bib70) and RuATD [Shamardina et al. (2022)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07940v1#bib.bib73) have provided datasets and encouraged centralized evaluation and competition. While many shared resources do a good job of covering multiple generative models and domains, few include adversarial attacks and none include variation in decoding strategy—occasionally even failing to list the strategy used. These datasets are insufficiently challenging and promote the inflated reports of detector accuracy.

![Refer to caption](2405.07940v1/dataset_overview.png)

Figure 2: An overview of the structure of the RAID dataset. We generate 2,000 continuations for every combination of domain, model, decoding, penalty, and adversarial attack. This results in roughly 6.2 million generations for testing. We then evaluate each detector on all pieces of generated text in the dataset.
