## 5 Model-Specific Anomaly: Increased Compression Under Voice-Preserving Prompting

While the main text reports that voice-preserving prompts reduce normalization magnitude by approximately 32% on average, one model shows a counterintuitive pattern: for Claude Sonnet, voice-preserving instructions are associated with greater stylometric compression rather than reduced compression.
