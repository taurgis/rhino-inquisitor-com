## Generate Your Own

Choose one of the four default policies below to generate your robots.txt directives:

Note: robots.txt files express your preferences. They do not, however, prevent crawler operators from taking your content at a technical level. Some crawler operators may disregard your robots.txt preferences and take your content regardless of what your robots.txt file says. Courts and regulators may conclude that robots.txt files do not impose enforceable legal obligations. For more information regarding your rights and the significance of robots.txt, consult a lawyer.

Policy

#### Disallow All

Most restrictive. Instructs crawler operators not to take your content for any purpose without your express, written permission. May cause search engines like Google to exclude your site from search results.

#### Allow Search Only

Instructs crawler operators only to use your site's contents for building a search index and providing search results (e.g., returning hyperlinks and short excerpts from your website's contents). Search does not include providing AI-generated search summaries. Does not give permission for AI Input and AI Training.

#### Allow Search & AI Input

Instructs crawler operators only to use your site's contents for building a search index, providing search results, and for AI Input. AI Input means inputting content into one or more AI models (e.g., retrieval augmented generation, grounding, or other real-time taking of content for generative AI search answers). This option does not give permission to train AI models on your content.

#### Allow Search, AI Input & AI Training

Permits crawler operators to use your site's contents for all of Search, AI Input, and AI Training.
