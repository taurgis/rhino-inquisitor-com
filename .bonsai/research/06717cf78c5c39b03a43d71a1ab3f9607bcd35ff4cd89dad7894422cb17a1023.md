### 3.4 Task Statistics

After task preparation, LongJudgeBench contains a diverse collection of long-form output evaluation instances across multiple scenarios and judging protocols. Compared with existing LLM-as-a-judge benchmarks, where candidate outputs are typically shorter than 500 tokens on average, LongJudgeBench targets substantially longer outputs, with an average length exceeding 9,000 tokens. Beyond increasing output length, these instances expose LLM judges to real-world document-level evaluation demands, such as assessing overall organization, cross-section consistency, and coverage and depth across multiple subtopics.
