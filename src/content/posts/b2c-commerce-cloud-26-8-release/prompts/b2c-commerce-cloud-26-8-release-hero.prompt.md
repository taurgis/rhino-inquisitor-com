# Image prompt: b2c-commerce-cloud-26-8-release-hero.png

## Purpose and placement

This is the **hero image** for the post "B2C Commerce 26.8: Unification, Anomalies, and Deadlines" (`src/content/posts/b2c-commerce-cloud-26-8-release/index.md`). It renders at the very top of the article, above the title/intro paragraph, and is used as the social-share/OG image. There is no surrounding prose or code it sits next to — it needs to work as a standalone visual summary of the post's three threads: catalog data flowing out to multiple destinations (Catalog Unification), a monitoring/anomaly-detection theme (Log Center), and a countdown/deadline theme (PWA Kit and Managed Runtime retirement dates).

## Subject

The site's mascot: an anthropomorphic gray rhinoceros. Stocky, humanoid proportions with an upright two-legged stance. Human-like five-fingered hands. A heavy sloped brow, small rounded ears, one large primary horn and one smaller secondary horn above the snout, and thick, wrinkled, textured gray skin rendered with visible linework (not smooth or plasticky). For this image, the rhino is dressed as a systems operator: sleeves rolled up or wearing a simple utility jacket/vest, standing at a control desk rather than in a suit — practical, hands-on, not corporate.

## Scene

The rhino stands at a wide control desk covered in glowing dashboard panels and readouts (charts, a small anomaly-alert icon, a search/magnifying-glass icon) — evoking monitoring and Log Center without needing to spell out any product name. Above or beside the desk, a single glowing data stream or feed line leaves the rhino's console and splits into several branching paths, each ending in a distinct simple icon: a small storefront/shop icon, a browser/web-window icon, and a generic terminal/POS-device icon — representing one catalog source syncing out to multiple destinations. Mounted on the desk or the wall behind the rhino is a large analog-style countdown clock or gauge with its hands/needle pushed toward a red zone, suggesting an approaching deadline. The rhino's posture is attentive and alert — one hand resting on the desk near the branching feed, the other gesturing toward or checking the countdown clock — as if actively watching multiple things at once, not passively standing.

## Setting

An interior control-room setting: dim ambient lighting with the desk and its dashboard panels as the main light sources, similar in mood to a server room or ops center. Soft rack-light bokeh or a darker gradient background behind the rhino keeps the focus on the desk and the branching feed. No windows or outdoor elements.

## Color palette

Warm, restrained "Paper & Ink" palette. Base tones are cream/paper midtones and warm grays for the rhino's skin and the desk. Deep ink-navy and charcoal shadows fill the background and recesses. Reserve a single warm amber/gold accent color for the one most important story element — the branching data feed and/or the countdown clock's warning glow — so it reads as the focal point at a glance. Avoid neon colors, saturated primary reds/blues/greens, and default AI-generator color grading (no teal-and-orange cinematic grade, no oversaturated rim lighting).

## Line and rendering style

Clean, dark ink outlines in a comic-book line-art style, filled with painterly digital shading and visible brushwork texture. Skin, fabric, and metal surfaces should show hand-painted texture and visible brush strokes, not flat vector fills or cel-shaded gradients. Not photorealistic — this is illustrated, not rendered like a photo.

## Composition

The rhino and control desk occupy the left-to-center portion of the frame, facing or angled toward the branching feed and icons. Leave open, relatively uncluttered negative space on the right third of the image (or wherever the busiest visual elements are not) so a text headline could be overlaid later without covering the rhino's face or the key icons. Landscape orientation, single cohesive scene — no panel splits, no collage, no side-by-side comparison frames.

## No-text constraint

Do not render any legible text, labels, numbers, logos, UI chrome with readable words, or watermarks anywhere in the image. Dashboard panels, gauges, and icons should be purely graphical/iconographic (bars, lines, a clock face, simple pictograms) with no readable captions, product names, or numerals that are meant to be read. Any exact wording or values belong in the article text, not in this artwork.

## Aspect ratio / output

16:9 landscape orientation, approximately 1672×941 pixels (matching this site's existing hero images). Single cohesive illustrated scene, high level of detail, no panel splits or collage.
