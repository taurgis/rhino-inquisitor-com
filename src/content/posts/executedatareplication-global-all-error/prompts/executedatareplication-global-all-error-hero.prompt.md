# Image prompt: executedatareplication-global-all-error-hero.png

**Purpose:** Hero image for the blog post "ExecuteDataReplication: Why "global": "all" Fails" on rhino-inquisitor.com (Salesforce B2C Commerce Cloud developer blog). It sits at the top of the article, as the front matter hero image, above the first paragraph. The post is about a job configuration (JSON) that Business Manager rejects with a schema error because a plain string was given where a curly-brace object is required.

**Output file name:** `executedatareplication-global-all-error-hero.png` (save next to the post's `index.md`).

## Prompt (self-contained, paste as-is)

Create a single, cohesive, high-detail illustration in a comic-book-ink-and-painted-digital style.

**Subject:** The site's mascot, an anthropomorphic gray rhinoceros: stocky, humanoid proportions, upright two-legged stance, human-like five-fingered hands, heavy sloped brow, small rounded ears, one large horn plus one smaller horn behind it, textured wrinkled gray skin. He is dressed as a casual software developer: a rolled-sleeve button-up shirt in muted navy, no hat, no coat. His expression is a frown of puzzled irritation, one eyebrow lowered, mouth slightly pulled to the side.

**Scene:** The rhino stands at a large, floating, semi-transparent configuration panel (a glass-like screen shown in cream and navy tones). On the panel, a plain rounded token (like a small pill-shaped tag, completely blank, no letters) sits on a slot that is clearly shaped for a pair of curly braces. The tag does not fit the braces-shaped slot, and a small jagged red-orange error mark radiates from the mismatch. Next to it, a second pair of curly braces glows in warm amber gold, holding a small blank toggle switch in the on position, hinting at the correct fix. The rhino holds the blank pill-shaped tag between thumb and forefinger, looking at it sceptically, while his other hand rests on his hip. The amber-gold curly braces are the single brightest, most saturated element in the whole picture.

**Setting:** A quiet, dim server-room-meets-developer-desk backdrop: soft out-of-focus rows of server racks and a desk lamp in deep ink-navy. Gentle warm key light from the left, as if from a desk lamp, with cool navy shadows on the right. Subtle floating paper-like particles; no clutter.

**Color palette:** Warm, restrained "Paper and Ink" palette. Cream and aged-paper midtones, deep ink-navy shadows, gray for the rhino. One warm amber/gold accent color, reserved exclusively for the glowing curly braces and their toggle. The error mark may be a muted brick red, small and secondary. No neon, no saturated primaries, no default AI-generator color grading, no electric blue glow.

**Line and rendering style:** Clean dark ink outlines (comic-book line art) filled with painterly digital shading and visible brushwork texture. Not flat vector or cel-shading, not photorealistic, not 3D render.

**Composition:** Landscape. Rhino on the right third of the frame, three-quarter view facing left toward the panel, head and hands clearly visible. The floating panel with the braces occupies the center. Keep the left third relatively open and uncluttered (soft dark navy and out-of-focus background) as negative space for a possible text overlay.

**No-text constraint:** Render no legible text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, UI captions or watermarks anywhere in the image. All screens, tags and panels show only abstract shapes, lines, symbols (curly braces are allowed as pure shapes) and blank areas.

**Aspect ratio / output:** 16:9 landscape, about 2000 x 1091 pixels (existing hero images on the site are 2000 x 1091). Single cohesive scene, high detail, no panel splits, no collage, no border or frame.
